    Welcome to ZF

    ZF is a global technology company supplying highly advanced dynamic systems for passenger cars, commercial vehicles and industrial technology. Its comprehensive product range is primarily aimed at vehicle manufacturers, mobility providers and start-up companies in the fields of transportation and mobility. ZF electrifies a wide range of vehicles types. With its products and the integrated hardware and software solutions, the company helps make mobility safer and smarter.

    Hotspot 3 Aftermarket

    Aftermarket

    As one of the leading companies in the aftermarket industry, ZF bridges the gap between today's and tomorrow's needs by delivering what workshops, distributors, and fleets require across the board.

    Our aftermarket product portfolio

    Alongside the automotive sector – passenger cars and commercial vehicles – ZF also serves market segments such as construction and agricultural machinery, wind power, marine propulsion, rail drives and test systems.

    With some 153,000 employees worldwide, ZF reported sales of €38.8 billion in fiscal year 2025. The company operates 162 production locations in 29 countries.
