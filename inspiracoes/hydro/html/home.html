Hydro's website, hydro.com, uses cookies.

Cookies are small text files that help make the site function properly and improve your experience. We use necessary cookies to enable the basic operation and security of the website. We also use optional cookies, with your consent, to remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, and to personalize content or ads.

Some cookies are placed by third‑party providers whose tools we use for security, analytics, or advertising. These third parties may combine information collected from your use of our site with other information you have provided to them or that they have collected from your use of their services. The third party listed as responsible for a third-party cookie is the Data Controller of the personal data collected by their respective cookies. About

Norsk Hydro ASA is the Data Controller for personal data collected through Hydro’s own cookies. The legal basis for using strictly necessary cookies is Hydro’s legitimate interest (GDPR article 6(1)(f)). The legal basis for all optional cookies is your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)), which you can change or withdraw at any time through the "Change your consent" link in the cookie banner. You may also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect how the website functions.

Cookies are stored only for as long as needed for their purposes, which will vary depending on the type of cookie. Modern browsers and devices handle cookie storage differently, and features such as possibility to restore previous browser sessions and manual deletion of cookies may interfere with the defined periods. The descriptions stated herein depicts how cookies on our website are designed to behave, but we cannot accurately account for variations in user-specific browser or device settings.

  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that are designed to only be active while you browse the website and are normally deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: Cookies that remain active and are stored on your device for a defined period. These are removed automatically once that period expires, if not manually deleted beforehand.

The term of each cookie can be found in the "Maximum Storage Duration" column of the cookie table below. All Hydro's own cookies have a defined maximum duration.

To learn more about how we process personal data and how to contact us, visit the Privacy Notice page. If you want to contact us specifically regarding your cookie consent, please state your consent ID and date.

    Aluminium
