Sustainable innovation

We believe that investing in advancing technology will increasingly be the primary path to pursuing a more sustainable future.
Learn more Close

Social responsibility and Human rights

Caring for people is a premise we extend to our entire value chain. Our efforts are divided into three main fronts: social responsibility; human rights; and diversity.
Learn more Close

Operational eco-efficiency

Eco-efficiency, for us, is to produce and provide competitive services to the market with a lower consumption of natural resources and lower production of pollutants. By doing so, companies can become more environmentally responsible and more profitable.
Learn more Close

Combating climate change

We reduced the intensity of the emissions of greenhouse gases by over 17% between 2008 and 2020, which prevented the emission of nearly 30 million tons of CO2 - the equivalent to planting more than 200 million trees. 
Learn more Close

Elimination of plastic waste

Our ambition is to be recognized worldwide as a company that develops the recycling supply chain in the regions where we operate and be the leaders in recycling in the Americas.
Learn more Close

Economic and financial results

We strive for economic and financial results based on a solid sustainable development strategy, continually strengthening ESG practices and on lasting relationships with our clients.
Learn more Close

Health and safety

Health and Safety are fundamental and non-negotiable values for Braskem. As part of our care for team members, we adopt safety practices to ensure the integrity and health of our teams.
