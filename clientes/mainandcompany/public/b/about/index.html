About us

A family business, machining since 1952

Main & Company was founded in June 1952 and has been in continuous operation by the founding family and long-time employees. All of the principals are actively engaged in the business.

We perform production machining on a contract basis, mainly for aerospace and other high-precision industries. Most of our parts call for highly skilled labor, good equipment and ingenious processing methods, in ferrous and non-ferrous materials, forgings, castings and bar stock, for aircraft, missile, submarine, spacecraft, medical and laboratory applications.

Our plant has about 12,500 sq ft of modern manufacturing and office space, about 1/4 mile north of the I-94 Cooper Street exit (Exit 139). Over the years we have grown modestly and strategically, while staying financially solid and a safe, rewarding workplace where many employees have built long careers.
