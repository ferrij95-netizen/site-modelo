Quality

Quality is our way of life

A quality system that began with MIL-Q-9858 in 1962 and is certified today to AS9100D with ISO 9001:2015.

High quality is driven by the management team and flows every day through the organization, beginning on the manufacturing floor, where every team member shares responsibility for the quality of our products. Our Quality Policy commits us to exceed our customers’ expectations.

Our environmentally controlled quality lab includes all standard mechanical measurement devices, from micrometers and optical comparators to thread, ring and plug gages and surface finish equipment, plus two DCC coordinate measuring machines (Zeiss Contura and Renishaw) with scanning, articulating heads that reach five sides of a part and automatic probe changers for unattended inspection.

Since 2007 our Quality Management System has been certified as a “Manufacturer of Precision Machined Components and Assemblies”. Because our niche is parts with complex shapes that are also hard to measure, we keep investing in measurement equipment, techniques and people.

  • AS9100D with ISO 9001:2015 (NSF-ISR)
  • Environmentally controlled quality lab
  • Two DCC CMMs with scanning and recording
  • First article inspection when required
