Capabilities

Capabilities

  1. 01

    CNC Turning

    Several CNC turning centers, from small 5,000 RPM machines for parts as small as 1/8 inch in diameter to 40 HP machines with a turning capacity of 25 inches in diameter by 44 inches long and 4 inch capacity through the spindle.

    • Ø1/8 in to Ø25 in
    • 44 in length
    • 4 in bar capacity
  2. 02

    CNC Milling

    Our milling equipment includes 3 axis, 4 axis and full 5 axis machining centers with size capacity up to 40 × 20 × 25 inches.

    • 3, 4 and 5 axis
    • 40 × 20 × 25 in envelope
  3. 03

    Hogouts from solid bar

    Our “out of the box” thinking lets us machine parts from solid bar stock that were previously made from expensive investment castings and forgings, often with very long lead times. The result is significant cost savings for our customers.

    • Replace castings and forgings
    • Shorter lead times
  4. 04

    Castings and forgings

    We have become specialists at machining small forgings and castings through unusual and unique methods of chucking and work holding for intricate shapes, from 80 lb castings to parts small enough for a pocket watch.

  5. 05

    CAD/CAM programming

    We keep up to date with the latest versions of AutoCAD and Mastercam, and work with a large variety of translators such as Catia, IGES, DWG and DXF.

  6. 06

    Special processing, turnkey

    Through long-time processing partners we supply complete parts and assemblies under single-source control.

    • Heat treat and induction
    • Grinding and lapping
    • Electroless nickel and chrome
    • Hard coat anodize
    • TiCN coating
    • Laser marking
    • MPI, FPI and X-ray

How a job moves through the shop

From print to shipped part

  1. 1

    Review

    We review your print, tolerances and quantities with you.

  2. 2

    Plan and program

    Process planning, fixturing and Mastercam programming.

  3. 3

    Machine

    CNC turning and 3 to 5 axis milling on site.

  4. 4

    Finish

    Heat treat, plating, coating and NDT through our partners.

  5. 5

    Inspect and ship

    DCC CMM inspection in a controlled lab, then delivery on time.
