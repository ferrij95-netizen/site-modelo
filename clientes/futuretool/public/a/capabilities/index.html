Capabilities

Capabilities

  1. 01

    Molds

    Mold design, manufacture, repair and consultation. We achieve optimum cycle time through proper gating, venting, material selection and adequate cooling, while keeping part integrity and aesthetics.

    • Plastic injection
    • Structural foam
    • Die cast
    • Vacuum
    • Prototype
  2. 02

    Reverse engineering of OEM parts

    We improve OEM parts through design changes, better material selection and surface treatments, to reduce downtime caused by failed or worn parts.

  3. 03

    Packaging machinery conversions

    Conversions and rebuilds of packaging machinery, plus design and manufacture of small custom machines.

  4. 04

    CNC machining, EDM and fabrication

    A complete machine shop with CNC machining capacity of 30 × 60 × 26 in, EDM and fabrication.

    • CNC 30 × 60 × 26 in
    • EDM
    • Fabrication
  5. 05

    In-house design

    Our designers use Inventor, Mastercam and AutoCAD and work closely with customers to support every service we offer.

    • Inventor
    • Mastercam
    • AutoCAD
