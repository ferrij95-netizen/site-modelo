About us

A precision machine shop in Rockford

Future Tool is a precision machine shop whose dedicated work force maintains quality workmanship throughout all stages of manufacturing. Molds, OEM parts, machine conversions and custom machines all go through the same careful process.

We are located in Rockford, Illinois, 80 miles west of Chicago and 90 miles southwest of Milwaukee, close to interstates I-90 and I-39 and only minutes from Chicago Rockford International Airport, home to a UPS overnight air hub. That means last-minute loading for rushed overnight jobs.

Why customers come back

  • Save money
