Titanium Bars / Billets

Hermith GmbH supplies precision-engineered titanium round bars tailored for high-performance technical applications. Thanks to their lightweight structure, exceptional corrosion resistance, and superior strength-to-weight ratio, these titanium bars deliver dependable results in demanding operating environments. They are commonly used in aerospace engineering, medical technology, marine systems, and a wide range of industrial applications where performance and material reliability are critical.

As an experienced manufacturer and distributor of titanium rods, Hermith GmbH supplies a comprehensive selection of unalloyed and alloyed titanium bar materials. The range includes commonly used grades such as Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 19 and 23, as well as customer-specific alloy compositions. Grade 5 titanium round bar (Ti-6Al-4V) remains the most popular option due to its excellent combination of high strength, durability, and manufacturing flexibility. All products are produced to tight dimensional tolerances and delivered with consistent quality in compliance with international specifications and individual technical requirements.

Combining exceptional strength, outstanding corrosion resistance, and low weight, they deliver superior performance wherever reliability, efficiency, and long-term durability are essential.

Titanium Plates / Sheets

Hermith GmbH supplies high-quality titanium sheets and plates for use in aerospace, industrial, medical, and other advanced sectors. Manufactured to meet demanding quality standards, these materials are suitable for applications including aircraft structures, medical implants, and high-performance industrial components, where titanium Grade 2 sheet and other alloys provide an excellent combination of mechanical strength and corrosion resistance. 

These titanium rolled sheets and plates are offered in Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 12, 23, as well as alloys such as Ti-6242, Ti-6246, and other specified compositions. They are produced in accordance with leading international specifications, including AMS 4911, AMS 4928, and ASTM B265, ensuring consistent quality and compliance for demanding applications.

Titanium Seamless Tubes

Hermith GmbH offers both seamless titanium tubes designed for demanding applications that require a combination of low mass, high mechanical strength, and high corrosion resistance. The portfolio covers Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 23, and additional titanium alloys.

Among them, Grade 9 tubes are especially preferred in aerospace structures due to their excellent strength-to-weight performance and forming characteristics. Hermith’s titanium tubes are engineered to main global standards, including AMS 4943, AMS 4944, AMS 4945, ASTM B338, ASTM B861, and other standards, ensuring full compliance with aerospace and industrial requirements.

Serving customers across Europe and the United States, Hermith GmbH supplies seamless titanium tubes for aerospace, industrial processing, and medical applications. These lightweight yet robust titanium tube solutions offer corrosion resistance and long-term durability, making them well suited for hydraulic systems, heat exchangers, and other high-performance assemblies.

Titanium Wire / Rod

Hermith GmbH produces and supplies titanium wire engineered for diverse technological applications such as additive manufacturing, 3D printing, welding processes, and medical device production, where material purity and consistent performance are essential. Offered in Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 12, 23, Ti-662, and additional titanium alloys, this corrosion-resistant, high-strength wire is trusted by aerospace, medical, and industrial customers around the world. Hermith’s titanium wire stock complies with major international standards, including AMS, ASTM, AWS, ISO, etc., with additional specifications available upon request. 

Hermith also offers customised wire surface finishes and coil configurations to suit specific process requirements, from continuous feeding for 3D printing to welding wire applications.

Titanium Special Alloys

Hermith GmbH offers a selected range of special titanium alloys developed for applications requiring enhanced strength, elevated temperature resistance, corrosion performance, and functional reliability. These alloys are used in demanding environments across aerospace, automotive, medical, additive manufacturing, and advanced industrial applications.

Our portfolio includes Ti 6242 and Ti 6246 for high-temperature, high-strength and high corrosion resistance applications, Ti-6Al-7Nb for biomedical use, and proprietary Hermith alloys designed for specialised functions. Hermith Sono-Spec is engineered for ultrasonic welding equipment, Hermith Spring Alloy is optimised for spring applications—particularly in the automotive sector—and Hermith 64 Additive Alloy is developed for additive manufacturing technologies.

All special alloys are produced in accordance with international standards or customer-specific requirements, ensuring reliable performance in critical applications.
