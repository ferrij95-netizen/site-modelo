Titanium Materials Available from Stock

Hermith offers a wide range of titanium materials available from stock, including bars, sheets, plates, tubes and wire in commercially pure titanium and titanium alloys. Stock material is stored in our Munich warehouse and can be prepared for collection or shipment to customers within 1–2 weeks after order confirmation.

Our stock range supports urgent requirements, small and medium-size projects, and regular supply needs across aerospace, medical, industrial and other high-performance sectors.

* Please note that stock availability may change depending on current orders. Contact our sales team to receive the latest stock list, dimensions, grades and delivery options.
