Hermith GmbH is a Munich-based German company and an experienced manufacturer and global supplier of titanium and titanium alloy products, serving demanding industries worldwide. We specialise in the production and supply of a wide range of titanium semi-finished products, including titanium round bars, rectangular bars, billets, plates, sheets, seamless and welded tubes, and titanium wire.

We supply certified titanium materials in common and specialised grades such as Grade 2 (CP Titanium), Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V), Grade 9 (Ti-3Al-2.5V), Grade 23 (Grade 5 ELI) and other high-performance titanium alloys. Our products are manufactured and delivered in accordance with international standards including AMS, ASTM, ISO, and EN, meeting strict technical and quality requirements for critical applications.

Serving customers across Europe, the USA, and international markets, we act as a reliable titanium materials producer and distributor for the aerospace, medical, automotive, additive manufacturing, chemical, energy, and advanced industrial sectors. Our product portfolio supports applications requiring high strength, corrosion resistance, low weight, and full material traceability.

We cover the entire titanium supply chain — from sourcing and processing to quality control, certification, and global logistics — and support OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, and specialised manufacturers with flexible and customer-oriented solutions. We supply both standard titanium stock and custom-engineered titanium products, including materials for aerospace structures, medical implants, additive manufacturing, and precision machining.

With a strong focus on certified quality, supply chain reliability, and long-term partnerships, we position ourselves as a trusted titanium supplier and one of the key players in the global titanium market.
