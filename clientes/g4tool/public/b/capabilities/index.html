Capabilities

Value engineering, CNC milling, CNC turning and wire EDM under one roof.

Our value engineering department brings prototyping, design, system planning and production together, so your part is designed to be made well and made affordably.

01

Value engineering

A full-range value engineering department with advanced capabilities in prototyping, design, system planning and production. We look for the simplest way to make your part right.

  • Prototyping
  • Design
  • System planning
  • Production
02

CNC milling

Takumi 3 and 4 axis vertical machining centers up to 12,000 RPM, plus a Haas toolroom mill and a Bridgeport for quick work.

  • Takumi V10A and V11A, 4 axis
  • Work space up to 20x40 in.
  • Haas TM-1 and Bridgeport
03

CNC turning

Hwacheon and Hyundai-Wia lathes with live tooling and bar capacity up to 2 1/2 in. for complete turned parts.

  • Live tooling, 3 axis
  • Bar up to 2 1/2 in.
  • 5C and QG65 systems
04

Wire EDM and support

A Makino U53 4-axis wire EDM with a 3,000 lb table capacity for precision profiles, plus Kalamazoo cold saws for material prep.

  • Makino U53 wire EDM
  • 18x24 in. work space
  • Kalamazoo cold saws
