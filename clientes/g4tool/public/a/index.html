Who we are

From the first idea to the production run.

G4 Tool & Technology is a machine shop in Elkhart, Indiana with a value engineering department that works with you on prototyping, design and system planning before a single chip is cut.

We serve mass transit, agricultural, RV, aftermarket, racing, plastic molding and marine customers, as well as builders of specialized machine tools. We are proud to be a certified Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE) and Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB).
