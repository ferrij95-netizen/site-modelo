About us

From an 800 sq ft barn to a 22,000 sq ft shop

L. E. Warren was founded by H. W. Warren in November 1941 as Warren Products. The company started with one employee in an 800 sq ft barn and found success as a supplier to larger manufacturers filling World War II needs.

In 1969 the company was bought by Leo Warren, and it was incorporated in 1974. L. E. Warren operates today in a well-equipped 22,000 sq ft facility with 20 employees, who average over 12 years seniority and more than 19 years of machining experience.
