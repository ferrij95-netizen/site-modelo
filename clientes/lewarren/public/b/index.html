Who we are

We have the design, machining experience and capability you need.

L. E. Warren was founded by H. W. Warren in November 1941 as Warren Products, with one employee in an 800 sq ft barn, supplying larger manufacturers during World War II.

Today we run a well-equipped 22,000 sq ft facility with 20 employees who average more than 12 years seniority and 19 years of machining experience. From customer-assisted design through complete manufacturing, we take on the special jobs: transfer lines, fixtures, molds, large chucks and one-off parts.
