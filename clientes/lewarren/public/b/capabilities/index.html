Capabilities

Capabilities

Piece weights from ounces to tons, versatility and fast turnaround.

Our CNC-equipped shop specializes in fast turnaround, with the capacity to handle one piece, prototype and short production runs. We provide emergency machining and repair, and in-house welding.

01

Machine design and prototypes

We use SolidWorks and Surfcam for our design services, from customer-assisted design through complete manufacturing. Molds, fixtures and first articles built to prove the idea.

  • SolidWorks
  • Surfcam
  • Customer-assisted design
02

Special machinery

Transfer lines, band saw roll lines, 45 ft bar transfer lines and 32 ft polisher bar feed roll lines, designed, built and delivered from our shop.

03

One-piece and short-run production

From a clamp assembly for a 60 in diameter saw to a 36 in fuel cell chuck, we machine single parts and short runs with piece weights from ounces to tons.

04

CNC machining

Hurco vertical machining centers, Doosan Puma turning centers, a Toyoda CNC lathe and a Lucas horizontal bore mill.

  • Milling
  • Turning
  • Boring
05

Machine repair and rebuild

Emergency machining and repair to get your line running again, plus complete machine rebuilds.

06

Welding and assembly

MIG, TIG, stick and oxyacetylene welding, and assembly services, so we can deliver a complete, tested unit.

  • MIG
  • TIG
  • Stick
  • Oxyacetylene
  • Assembly
