Frigoestrela quality

Every department, from farm to consumer

Frigoestrela is continuously dedicated to producing high-standard meat, with every department involved, from buying the animals to serving the consumer.

Meat quality depends on safety procedures and standards covering animal welfare, sanitary care, nutrition and sustainability.

  • SIFFederal Inspection Service in every unit
  • BPF · PSOGMP and SSOP in the whole operation
  • Animal welfareFrom the farm to the plant
Processes

How quality is built, step by step

  1. 01

    Farm partnerships

    Our Cattle Purchasing team runs strict audits and selects farms that meet animal welfare, breeding, pre-slaughter handling and transport requirements.

  2. 02

    Transport and welfare

    Transport to our units follows animal welfare best practices, including unloading and handling before production.

  3. 03

    MAPA self-control

    The whole production line runs self-control programs required and inspected by the Ministry of Agriculture (MAPA) and by importing countries.

  4. 04

    Good Manufacturing Practices

    Every plant employee is trained in GMP, which guides personal, equipment, facility and common-area hygiene and care in production every day.

  5. 05

    Sanitary procedures

    The entire operation follows Sanitation Standard Operating Procedures (SSOP).

  6. 06

    Accredited laboratories

    Continuous improvement programs and integrated solutions with accredited laboratories, hygiene standards and high-tech processes.
