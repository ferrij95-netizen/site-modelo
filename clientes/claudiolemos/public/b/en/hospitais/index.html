Hospitals

Why operate in a hospital?

There is a common misconception that plastic surgery is different from conventional surgery. Just as nobody would picture heart surgery in an ordinary office, plastic surgery deserves the same standard. Even with every safety measure, a complication can happen, and in a hospital the response is immediate.

What a hospital guarantees

  • Professionals from every area of health on site, ready to act in an emergency.
  • Lower risk of infection, with instruments sterilised to standard.
  • Hospitals that are already a reference in healthcare in Rio de Janeiro.
  • More comfort and safety for the patient throughout the procedure.
  • Full compliance with health surveillance (Anvisa) requirements.
