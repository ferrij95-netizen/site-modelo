Dr. Cláudio Lemos

Training, societies and approach

Dr. Cláudio Lemos is an aesthetic plastic surgeon, a specialist and full member of the Brazilian Society of Plastic Surgery. He is also an active member of the Association of Former Students of Prof. Ivo Pitanguy, the International Society of Plastic Surgery, the American Society of Plastic Surgeons and the Brazilian College of Surgeons.

I had the honour of being a student of Professor Ivo Pitanguy, a world reference who shaped the way I see surgery.

Dr. Cláudio Lemos

Advanced technology is an ally, but it only makes sense when applied with judgement, safety and experience.

Every plan is individual, careful and focused on a natural look, safety and harmony.

Every patient is followed with technical responsibility, judgement and individual attention.

The surgeon
