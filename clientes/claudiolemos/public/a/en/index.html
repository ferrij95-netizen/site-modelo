Dr. Cláudio Lemos

Dr. Cláudio Lemos is an aesthetic plastic surgeon, a specialist and full member of the Brazilian Society of Plastic Surgery. He is also an active member of the Association of Former Students of Prof. Ivo Pitanguy, the International Society of Plastic Surgery, the American Society of Plastic Surgeons and the Brazilian College of Surgeons.

Hospitals

Surgery performed in leading Rio de Janeiro hospitals

Every plastic surgery, aesthetic or reconstructive, should take place in a hospital, with a medical team on call and full facilities. It is not a luxury: it is safety.

  • Hospital Copa Star

    Copacabana. A high-end hospital with an experience comparable to a five-star hotel.

  • Hospital Copa D’Or

    Copacabana. One of the best-known hospitals in Rio’s South Zone.

  • Casa de Saúde São José

    Founded in 1923, one of the city’s most traditional institutions.

  • Hospital Perinatal

    A hospital with more than 30 years of history in Rio de Janeiro.

  • Clínica São Vicente

    In Gávea since 1933, a pioneer in several fields of medicine.

  • Hospital Badim

    A hospital with a state-of-the-art surgical centre.
