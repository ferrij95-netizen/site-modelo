About us

Grupo Toniolo

To make the business a national and international reference in its field, becoming a company allied with the environment and committed to clients, suppliers and employees. This is the foundation of Grupo Toniolo’s pioneering story, which began in 1980 in the town of Colombo, in the state of Paraná.

Always adapting to the needs and changes of this competitive market, the company has spent its years looking for creative solutions, tailoring its actions, products and services. We have developed unique, exclusive techniques, the best known being tailings relocation with our patented amphibious excavators, which has given many dams more safety and a longer service life.

Solutions

Nine fields of work for mining, dams and water bodies.

All solutions
  1. 01 Tailings management and relocationReorganizing tailings inside the dam itself with patented amphibious excavators.
  2. 02 Dam stability and decharacterizationControlled tailings removal, new geometry and drainage for safer structures.
  3. 03 Tailings dewateringMechanized acceleration of tailings thickening, with water reuse.
  4. 04 Suction and discharge dredgingDredges, motor pumps and pumps sized for the flow of each removal project.
  5. 05 Precision dredgingAmphibious excavator with dredge pump and disaggregator: high solids, low turbidity.
  6. 06 Drying and dry stackingDry Stacking: from dewatering to the tailings stack, reusing up to 95% of the water.
  7. 07 Mechanized conveyor belt cleaningSabre attachment: cleaning under the belt with no manual work and no conveyor stop.
  8. 08 Macrophyte removalAmphibious excavator with attachments to cut and collect vegetation, branches and logs.
  9. 09 Mechanized removalSpecial equipment with booms up to 25 metres and the largest load capacity in Brazil.
