Grupo Toniolo

Pioneer in Brazil in amphibious excavator solutions.

Watch the video

To make the business a national and international reference in its field, becoming a company allied with the environment and committed to clients, suppliers and employees. This is the foundation of Grupo Toniolo’s pioneering story, which began in 1980 in the town of Colombo, in the state of Paraná.

Always adapting to the needs and changes of this competitive market, the company has spent its years looking for creative solutions, tailoring its actions, products and services. We have developed unique, exclusive techniques, the best known being tailings relocation with our patented amphibious excavators, which has given many dams more safety and a longer service life.

With this technique, tailings placement in the dam is controlled and planned, ensuring water quality and increasing the mechanical stability of the tailings, so that no dam we have worked on has ever entered a safety alert level.
