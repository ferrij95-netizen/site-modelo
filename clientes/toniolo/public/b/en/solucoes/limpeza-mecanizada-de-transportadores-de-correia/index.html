Mechanized conveyor belt cleaning

Sabre attachment: cleaning under the belt with no manual work and no conveyor stop.

Conveyor belt cleaning is essential in mining for operational efficiency, accident prevention and product quality. Clean belts reduce material losses, cut component wear, avoid contamination and last longer, which lowers replacement and repair costs.

To replace manual work in this task, Grupo Toniolo developed the Sabre attachment, mounted on a small excavator. It drags the sediment deposited under the belt to a loading point, without putting the conveyor structure at risk.

What it delivers

  • Works vertically and horizontally
  • Reaches restricted spaces
  • No need to stop the conveyor
  • No manual work under the belt
