Company

Burmann Alimentos began its work in 1991, mainly focused on representing small grain producers and cooperatives, selling their products to large distributors, supermarkets and food manufacturers. Today it operates as a grain broker and also as an importer and exporter of several foods.

It is located in Porto Alegre, capital of Rio Grande do Sul, the southernmost region of Brazil. Its climate differs from the rest of the country, with well-defined seasons, and it has some of the best soils for growing cereals, seeds and fruits such as wheat, rice, beans, sunflower seeds, corn and popcorn.

  • Grain broker
  • Importer
  • Exporter

Mission

To be a marketing channel for grain and food from the producer to distribution and manufacturing centers, providing quality food to our Brazilian and foreign customers and partners.

Vision

To be a national reference in the grain and food market and to increase our share of the international market.

Values

  • Ethical and responsible conduct.
  • Clear and precise communication.
  • Solid and reliable partnerships.
