From producer to customer

How Burmann works as a grain broker.

  1. 01

    Producers and cooperatives

    Small grain producers and cooperatives from Rio Grande do Sul and other regions.

  2. 02

    Brokerage

    Burmann represents the crop, negotiates volume, price and terms and follows every lot.

  3. 03

    Product selection

    Specially selected products, including for the international market.

  4. 04

    Customer

    Distributors, supermarkets and food manufacturers in Brazil and abroad.
