Dental aesthetics

A more beautiful, harmonious smile

Aesthetic dentistry restores the beauty of your smile with whitening, resin or porcelain restorations, gum surgery (gingivectomy) and modern dental veneers. ImplArt uses Digital Dentistry in every treatment.

Dental veneers and porcelain laminates

Dental veneers are ultra-thin porcelain shells bonded to the tooth surface with little or no tooth reduction. They correct:

  • Dark or white stains
  • Small fractures or chips
  • Asymmetries
  • Irregular shape or size
  • Gaps between teeth (diastema)

Porcelain is biosafe and similar to natural teeth in texture, color and translucency. At ImplArt, veneers are computer designed and produced with 3D printers.

Orofacial harmonization

Balance between aesthetics and function

Orofacial harmonization balances aesthetics and oral function, for a more youthful look. It is regulated by the Brazilian Federal Council of Dentistry (Resolution 198/2019).

  • Buccal fat removal

    Surgery to slim the face by reducing the cheeks.

  • Lip filler

    With hyaluronic acid, it balances the shape of the lips with the face.

  • Nasolabial folds

    Softens the line that runs from the nose to the corner of the mouth.

  • Double chin treatment

    With deoxycholic acid, the so-called enzymatic double chin lipolysis.
