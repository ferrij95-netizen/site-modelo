Treatments

Our specialties

01

Dental implants

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root placed in the jaw bone by the implantologist. The surgery is minimally invasive and replaces one missing tooth, several teeth or even a full arch.

02

Dental prosthesis

Translucent zirconia allows stronger, more natural fixed prostheses. We pioneered translucent multilayer full-contour zirconia bridges in Brazil.

03

Aesthetics and harmonization

Aesthetic dentistry restores the beauty of your smile with whitening, resin or porcelain restorations, gum surgery (gingivectomy) and modern dental veneers. ImplArt uses Digital Dentistry in every treatment.

04

Bone graft

Dental bone grafts are not necessarily large or invasive. In most cases they are simple and small, done to solve bone loss with maximum efficiency and minimum invasiveness.

Step by step

How implants work at ImplArt

  1. 1

    Assessment and CT scan

    Clinical exam, X-ray and CT scan in the clinic's own radiology center.

  2. 2

    Digital planning

    The implant is planned on the computer, in the position of the future prosthesis.

  3. 3

    Guided surgery

    Computer-guided, minimally invasive implant surgery that can be done in Day Clinic.

  4. 4

    Osseointegration

    The implant heals and bonds to the bone. Straumann implants heal faster.

  5. 5

    New tooth

    A crown or prosthesis made in the digital lab, similar to a natural tooth in chewing and looks.
