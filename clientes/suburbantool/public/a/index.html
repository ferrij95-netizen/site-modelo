Who we are

Uncompromising precision. Unrivaled commitment.

Suburban Tool & Die Co., Inc. is a full-service designer and manufacturer of quality, custom-built molds for the molding industry. We serve molders and OEMs in the automotive, consumer, electrical, medical and industrial markets.

With more than forty years in tooling and molding, we understand the importance of time to market and staying on budget, and we are committed to delivering tooling on time, to specification, every time.

Capabilities

Engineering, mold building and repair

All capabilities
  1. 01

    Engineering services

    Part design, design review for manufacturability and reverse engineering, followed by complete tooling design for every molding process we support.

    • Part design
    • Design for manufacturability
    • Reverse engineering
    • Unscrewing, two-shot, compound slide
    • Hot manifold, three plate
    • Book molds for natural rubber
    • Aluminum and zinc die design
  2. 02

    Mold building

    Complete mold building services with mold bases produced in house, replacement inserts and components, and prototype parts.

    • Mold bases in house
    • Replacement inserts
    • Prototype parts (FDM, SLA)
  3. 03

    Machining and EDM

    High-speed milling (Mazak Nexus 530C-II HS), turning, wire and ram EDM and precision grinding, for our molds and for custom and short-run machining.

    • High-speed milling
    • Wire and ram EDM
    • Precision grinding
    • Short-run machining
