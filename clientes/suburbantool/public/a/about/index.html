About us

Two generations of mold building

Our founders started the Suburban companies as a small tool shop in McKean, Pennsylvania. Since then, Suburban has established two mold-building companies: Suburban Tool & Die in Erie and Suburban Precision Mold in Meadville.

As of 2010 the second generation of ownership brings more than 65 years of combined experience with the company. Together with our sister company, we keep growing and evolving to meet, and exceed, the needs of our customers.

Our project management system monitors and reports the progress of every project, from concept design to shipment, and we furnish status reports on request. We are close to Interstates 79 and 90 and only five minutes from Erie International Airport.
