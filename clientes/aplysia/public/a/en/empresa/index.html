Nice to meet you, we are APLYSIA.

A company that believes in what it does, and does it with competence and responsibility. A company that believes in sustainable progress and that every form of life and the environment deserve to be protected and preserved. A company that combines tradition and innovation in everything it does.

Speaking of innovation, we carry out projects using the most modern, internationally accepted techniques and methodologies. We also implement customized solutions and conduct research on biomonitoring and environmental control, working in 15 Brazilian states and several countries.

This story began a long time ago. Since 1997, we have developed complete solutions for industry. We are specialists in environmental assessment, identifying the causes and effects of impacts on the environment, with the know-how to identify, analyze and present solutions that bring efficiency gains and cost reductions to companies.

To this end, APLYSIA has a highly qualified team in engineering, biology, oceanography, geology and ecology, as well as an international network of specialized consultants, to plan, monitor, assess and license industrial and dredging activities, water and soil environmental management, among others.

We have our own laboratory for microbiological and ecotoxicological analyses, accredited to NBR ISO 17025, and we serve the most diverse sectors of the economy, giving their managers tools for greater agility, problem-solving and assertiveness.

We are able to do our best for your company, with different solutions and always striving for excellence. If you want peace of mind, count on us.

Practical results that comply with legislation and can bring efficiency gains and cost reductions to companies.
