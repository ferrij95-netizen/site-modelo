Monitoring

Terrestrial, aquatic and oceanographic, with management reports.

HomeServicesMonitoring

Environmental control requires follow-up according to technical, scientific and legal indicators. This includes monitoring what is discharged into the environment, through the assessment of waste and effluents.

APLYSIA offers different types of monitoring and can also analyze and recommend improvement and minimization actions when needed, integrating the information into management reports that communicate clearly with managers and regulatory agencies.

We also carry out special monitoring under the Environmental Effects Monitoring Protocol (2015), used for effluent monitoring and water resource classification in North America and Europe.

We verify that your company’s operations comply with the legal limits set by the CONAMA Resolutions for soil, water quality, effluents and dredging. APLYSIA is also qualified to carry out the rescue of fauna and flora that must be protected.

01

Environmental Monitoring (terrestrial and aquatic)

Integrated environmental monitoring and delivery of the solution for your company.

02

Active and passive monitoring of bioaccumulative compounds

Assess the quality of the water body receiving your company’s effluent with bioindicators, replacing numerous chemical analyses of the water.

03

Fauna and Flora Rescue

The fauna and flora rescue industries need before starting their activities.

04

Compliance with CONAMA Resolutions 357/2005, 420/2009, 430/2011, 454/2012 and others

APLYSIA can advise on matters related to your company’s environmental control.

05

Oceanographic Monitoring

High performance in physical, chemical, biological and geological monitoring of the marine environment.
