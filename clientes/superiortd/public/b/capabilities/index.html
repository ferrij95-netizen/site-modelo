Capabilities

Capabilities

A full-service tool and die company that meets the rigid standards of the aerospace and defense industries.

01

Tooling, dies and fixtures

Blanking, piercing and progressive dies, new or rebuilt, for small or large applications. Jigs and fixtures for large and small part holding in welding and machining, and comparative and measuring gages.

  • Progressive dies
  • Die rebuilds
  • Weld and machining fixtures
  • Gages
02

Contract machining

Manual and CNC turning up to 20 × 100 in, large manual mills up to 94 × 110 in and CNC milling up to 105 × 315 in, plus Blanchard grinding of parts up to 70 in diameter.

  • CNC milling 105 × 315 in
  • Turning 20 × 100 in
  • Blanchard 70 in
03

Special machines and equipment

Special machines and equipment built to your demanding specifications, including robotic and PLC-controlled applications.

  • Robotic applications
  • PLC-controlled fixtures
  • Hydraulic test equipment
04

Engineering

Build to print or custom engineered. We take total responsibility for design-and-build programs, working with strategic engineering partners and Pro/E.

05

Rebuilds and reconditioning

Large contract machining projects such as reconditioning a C-frame mechanical press, handled with our 26 ft clear height and 50,000 lb crane capacity.
