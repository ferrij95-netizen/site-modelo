About us

From 600 sq ft in 1952 to 38,000 sq ft today

Since 1952 the Hershberger family has maintained Superior Tool & Die Company’s reputation as a quality supplier of special equipment, CNC machining, fixtures and dies. We have grown from about 600 sq ft to 38,000 sq ft, with 28,000 sq ft of manufacturing area and a 26 ft clear height.

With a third generation in training, we plan to keep expanding our capabilities long into the future. Several employees have more than 40 years of experience, and the whole staff averages 24 years.

We have a history of internal training and “growing our own employees”, and we support St. Joseph County 4-H and school-to-work programs as a community partner.
