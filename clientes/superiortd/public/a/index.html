Who we are

Three generations of quality, on-time delivery and integrity.

Since 1952 the Hershberger family has kept Superior Tool & Die’s reputation as a quality supplier of special equipment, CNC machining, fixtures and dies. Our primary goal is to meet or exceed the requirements of our customers.

Our skilled employees average 24 years of experience, several with more than 40, and receive ongoing training for continuous quality improvement. Provide quality products, deliver them on time and conduct all business with integrity: that is our commitment to every customer.
