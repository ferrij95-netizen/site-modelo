About us

A Canton family shop since 1945

H-W Machine was founded in Canton, Ohio in 1945 and has been family owned ever since. Today a third generation runs the shop, still following the philosophy it started with: the best possible service at the fairest, most affordable cost.

We are a team of dedicated employees working together to provide cost-effective services. We sponsor federally approved apprenticeship programs, and we are always looking for new machinists to join us.
