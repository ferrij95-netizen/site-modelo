Capabilities

Services

From short-run production and prototypes to replacement parts and rebuilt equipment.

H-W Machine operates as a job shop. Our main focus is the steel mill and paper mill industries, and we keep investing in equipment and skilled people to meet the growing demands of other industries too.

01

General and large-part machining

Lathes, large-capacity turning equipment, and vertical and horizontal boring mills for parts big and small, in steel, alloy steels, stainless, aluminum and exotic metals.

  • Large-capacity turning
  • Vertical boring mills
  • Horizontal boring mills
02

Wedge-wire screen baskets

We produce wedge-wire screen baskets used in the pulp and paper industry.

  • Pulp and paper screens
  • New and replacement baskets
03

Roll repair

Repair of extended-length rolls used in steel making and of rolls used in paper mills, so they go back into service instead of being replaced.

  • Steel mill rolls
  • Paper mill rolls
  • Extended lengths
04

Reverse engineering and rebuilds

We take an existing or worn-out part, make drawings and duplicate it. Ideal for obsolete machines, and for anyone restoring antique motorcycles, tractors and cars. Rebuilding and refurbishing existing parts saves our customers significant cost.

  • Drawings from worn parts
  • Obsolete machine parts
  • Rebuild and refurbish
05

3-D CAD/CAM design and project development

Original designs or retrofit projects, product research and development, and equipment upgrades. We work with you to decide the best plan of attack for your project.

  • 3-D CAD/CAM
  • Retrofits and upgrades
  • Research and development
