Connecting Rod Boring and Grinding Machine RBF 500

FAMOP – Fábrica de Máquinas Operatrizes Ltda. – developed the Connecting Rod Boring and Grinding Machine within the strictest concepts of product engineering, where the high technological degree results in high productivity and accuracy, combined with excellent finishing.

The machine is easy to operate because it features two headstocks, one for machining and the other for grinding, built in closed cast-iron housings with a modern roller bearing system.

The base is a single piece of hardened and ground special cast iron, and the headstocks are scraped to assure perfect alignment, so that after boring the rod the wheel can start grinding with no need for further centering.

The table and the headstocks are driven by an electric set with four motors, three of them double-speed, assuring minimum maintenance costs.

Technical specifications and accessories

A single setup

The headstocks provide perfect alignment, so machining and grinding can be done in a single setup, for higher productivity.
