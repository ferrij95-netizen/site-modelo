Air Float Cylinder Boring Fixture MF 01

FAMOP has developed a Cylinder Boring Fixture that simplifies the boring of blocks and liners.

Manufactured in one single piece of cast iron, it is ground so as to maintain a perfect alignment between the bed and the supporting cradles.

The air float plate is equipped with a pneumatic piston for locking and an air cushion for cross and lengthwise movement.

Designed for any model and size of boring bar, it allows easy removal of the machine for external jobs.
