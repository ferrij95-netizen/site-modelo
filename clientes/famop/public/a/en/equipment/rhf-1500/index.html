Crankshaft Grinders RHF 1500 / RF 1500

FAMOP Crankshaft Grinders were developed with all the refinement of our technology. They are simple to operate, with a high degree of precision and speed.

The RHF 1500 version is equipped with a hydraulic system to move the wheel head and an electric system to move the table. All components are rigorously inspected. The machine geometry is checked through practical tests with crankshafts of various types and sizes.

Technical specifications and accessories

Hydraulic system (RHF 1500)

Fitted with an exclusive safety device that only allows the table to move when the wheel is fully away from the crankshaft, keeping operator and machine safe.

Base

A single piece of special cast iron, stress-relieved, ground and reinforced with cross and lengthwise ribs for stability, smooth movement and freedom from vibration.

FAMOP MAF-40 Micrometric Comparator

Made to follow the grinding job. Used while grinding, it shows the final size of the journal and its ovality with precision.

With hydraulic lift and side movement, it checks taper without moving the machine table.

Reading in 0.01 mm or 0.001", with a capacity of 9.5 to 127 mm or 3/8" to 5".
