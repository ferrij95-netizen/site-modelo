Company

FAMOP – Fábrica de Máquinas Operatrizes Ltda. – was established in 1964. Since then FAMOP has specialized in the manufacture of engine rebuilding machines. Today it offers modern, versatile machines with low maintenance costs and high productivity, the result of its advanced technological degree.

FAMOP’s rigid quality control subjects its products to intensive testing, which assures the quality of its equipment.

As a result of the great performance and high accuracy of the machines manufactured by FAMOP, several countries have already acquired equipment with this brand.

Designers specialized in precision machines assure the quality and development of new projects. Modern numeric control equipment allows higher component accuracy.
