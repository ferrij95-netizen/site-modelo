RF 1000

Crankshaft Grinder

Crankshaft grinder with 1,000 mm between chucks, closed headstock and speed variator.

  • Electronic vibration analysis on every unit
  • Chucks with 360° rotation
  • 26" grinding wheel

DescriptionTechnical specificationsStandard accessories

FAMOP has developed Crankshaft Grinders to meet all needs and requirements. Our machines are strongly built, highly efficient and precise, and have been designed in accordance with the latest technology.

All machine components are rigorously inspected and each unit produced is submitted to electronic vibration analysis and geometrical checking, and is tested with crankshafts of various sizes and types.

Headstock

  • Closed cast-iron housing with permanent lubrication of the spindles.
  • Adjustable white-metal bearings for a better ground finish.
  • Cross adjustment to correct parallelism.
  • Modern chuck system allowing horizontal and vertical centering and 360° rotation, fast and precise.
  • Fitted with a speed variator.

FAMOP MAF-40 Micrometric Comparator

Made to follow the grinding job. Used while grinding, it shows the final size of the journal and its ovality with precision.

With hydraulic lift and side movement, it checks taper without moving the machine table.

Reading in 0.01 mm or 0.001", with a capacity of 9.5 to 127 mm or 3/8" to 5".

Optional accessory: FAMOP LF 050 Portable Belt Polisher
