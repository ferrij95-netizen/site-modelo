DescriptionTechnical specificationsParts and accessories

With more than 30 years dedicated to the manufacture of engine rebuilding machines, FAMOP developed the Portable Cylinder Boring Bar within the strictest concepts of product engineering, combining quality and high performance.

The Portable Cylinder Boring Bar is easily operated and stands out for the precision and variety of work performed on light or heavy engine blocks and liners.

The main body is a single piece of treated cast iron, with ribs sized to ensure endurance, stability and a high finish of the machined surface.

It features two boring speeds, adjustable travel stop and fast automatic return, as well as a centering device based on three points of contact and a clamping device, both very fast and efficient.

Three-point centering

Fast centering based on three points of contact. Adjustable travel stop to suit the part being machined. Fast automatic return.
