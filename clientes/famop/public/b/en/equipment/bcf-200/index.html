Column Cylinder Boring Machine

Column-type cylinder boring machine in cast iron, for light and heavy engine blocks.

FAMOP has developed a Cylinder Boring Machine using the most recent concepts of cylinder boring. Built in cast iron, with a high degree of stability, allowing for excellent machining performance.

An equipment of simple operation that allows for precision and speed.

Equipped with an electric system for cutting speed variation and simultaneous feed. Automatic return with travel regulation ruler.
