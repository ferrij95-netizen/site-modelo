From block to crankshaft

A FAMOP machine for each step of engine rebuilding.

  1. 01

    Bore the cylinders

    Block and liners get back to size and geometry: BCF 200 column or MPF portable boring bar.

    BCF 200MPF 01-0
  2. 02

    Position the block

    The MF 01 air float fixture glides the block on an air cushion and locks it in place.

    MF 01
  3. 03

    Recondition the rods

    The RBF 500 bores and grinds the rod eye in a single setup.

    RBF 500
  4. 04

    Grind the crankshaft

    RF 1000, RF 1500 or RHF 1500, with the MAF-40 comparator measuring the journal while grinding.

    RF 1000RHF 1500
  5. 05

    Polish the shafts

    Final finish with the LF 050 portable belt polisher.

    RF 1000

Tested before it leaves the factory

FAMOP’s rigid quality control subjects its products to intensive testing, which assures the quality of its equipment.

  • Component inspection. All components are rigorously inspected.
  • Vibration analysis. Every grinder goes through electronic vibration analysis.
  • Geometry checked. Geometrical checking of the assembled machine.
  • Machining test. Practical tests with parts of various sizes and types.
