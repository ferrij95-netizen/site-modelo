Technical assemblies

Pre-assembled units, straight to the assembly line

Besides machining and welding, we develop, assemble and supply technical assemblies based on the customer's product. With in-house processes and a chain of about 50 suppliers, we deliver an integrated solution: cutting, bending, injection, casting, finishing, treatment, assembly, machining and bonding.

  • Assured quality

Industries

Three industries that demand precision and on-time delivery

Agricultural equipment

The agricultural industry is always looking for technologies and processes that cut costs. We supply parts and assemblies for assembly lines and spare parts for tractors and agricultural implements.

Petrochemical equipment

A fast-developing industry that is always seeking new equipment and technology. We supply machined parts and assemblies with the quality and safety this industry requires.

Machinery and tools

We supply serial and non-serial parts, with or without heat and surface treatment. CNC machines and up-to-date cutting tools let us make simple to complex parts with tighter form and position tolerances.

Quality

Paesi has been ISO 9001 certified since 2004, received a sector development award from Sebrae and takes part in the PGQP quality assessment system. From AGCO, it received the Top Quality Award (2003) and the ACRE cost reduction award (2007 and 2008).
