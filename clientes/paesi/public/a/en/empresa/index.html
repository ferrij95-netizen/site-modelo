Company

From oil and gas machining to assemblies for industry.

Paesi has state-of-the-art turning centers that combine turning, milling, drilling and threading in a single stage. For the customer, that means more productivity, efficiency and quality in machined parts.

Paesi has been ISO 9001 certified since 2004, received a sector development award from Sebrae and takes part in the PGQP quality assessment system. From AGCO, it received the Top Quality Award (2003) and the ACRE cost reduction award (2007 and 2008).

History

  1. 1997

    Founded in April to provide fast, quality precision machining and fitting for the oil and gas supply chain in Rio Grande do Sul.

  2. 2000

    In March, a partnership with a multinational agricultural equipment maker to supply technical assemblies.

  3. 2004

    In December, Paesi focuses exclusively on producing and assembling technical parts and assemblies for industry. ISO 9001 certification.

  4. 2005

    In January, moves to new headquarters, also in São Leopoldo: proper lighting, assembly cells, Kanban inventory control and a switch to state-of-the-art CNC machines.

  5. Today

    With its supplier chain, it offers assembly and machining of parts and assemblies involving cutting, welding, stamping, plastic injection, tooling, electrostatic and KTL painting, and bonding for elastomer injection or pressing.

Vision

To be a reference in Brazil's metalworking industry in machining, parts manufacturing and technical assemblies.

Mission

To provide integrated solutions for the metalworking industry with constant commitment to competitiveness, quality and trust, fostering the growth of customers, partners, employees and suppliers.
