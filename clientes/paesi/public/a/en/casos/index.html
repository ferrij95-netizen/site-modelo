Case studies

Real customer problems and how machining solved them.

01 · Machinery and tools

Traction shaft machining

The shaft drives and expands the tire during manufacturing. Any parallelism variation in the shaft, especially in the knurling, can shift the tire or throw it off the machine.

02 · Agricultural equipment

Hydraulic drive shaft for tractors

Because it goes into a wide range of machines, the drive shaft has to be produced continuously and in large quantities.

03 · Machinery and tools

Machining hardened D6 tool steel

The customer had quality and cost problems with the supply of vanes. Suppliers struggled to machine the material because of its hardness and strength, which drove up cycle time and tooling costs.

04 · Agricultural equipment

Shackle assembly

When assembling sub-assemblies for a tractor maker, problems found during assembly stop the line, with no time left to solve them.

05 · Agricultural equipment

Tube fitting machining

Done on multi-slide CNC lathes straight from stainless bar stock, which cuts idle time between parts.

06 · Agricultural equipment

Spacer tube machining

A part used in assemblies for agricultural machinery. Machined on a multi-slide CNC lathe from 4 m tubes, and it comes off the machine finished.

07 · Agricultural equipment

Insert for rubber with internal thread

Produced from 4 m bars on a multi-slide CNC lathe. Several tools cut at the same time, which greatly reduces production time.
