01

Turning with driven tools

With driven-tool CNC machines and an indexing chuck, we perform simultaneous precision turning and milling in a single process. Less setup, shorter production times and lower cost for complex parts, serial or not.

Technology

Feeler FTC350 XL CNC lathe, Fanuc control, CNC bar feeder and indexing chuck. Programming in SolidWorks CAM.

Advantage

Four axes bring turning, milling, drilling and threading together on one machine: fewer machines to set up, less preset time and more accuracy. The indexing chuck allows angled milling and drilling on the face and side.

Benefits

  • High machining speed
  • Excellent machining quality
  • Lower production costs
  • Fast setup and programming
  • Less downtime
  • Better use of material
  • High dimensional accuracy
  • Less preset, fewer machines

02

CNC milling

Serial and non-serial CNC milling of ferrous and non-ferrous materials, even after heat treatment. Cast housings, shafts, tools, keys, flanges and parts for the agricultural, oil and machinery industries.

Technology

Feeler FV1000 machining center with Mitsubishi M730 control. Programming and simulation lab with CAD, CAE and CAM: scrap and collisions are avoided before the machine is set up.

Advantage

Up-to-date tooling, accessories and software ensure quality, versatility and speed in large, medium and small batches.

04

Multi-slide CNC lathe (up to 60 mm)

Multi-slide CNC lathe with automatic bar feeding for series production. Bushings, shafts and pins machined from bar stock in ferrous materials, brass and aluminum. It currently serves the machinery and agricultural implement industries.

Technology

A machine built to Paesi's requirements, with a CNC control programmed manually or by computer, and automatic bar feeding.

Advantage

Medium and large batches with less downtime thanks to automatic feeding. Low operating cost, good machining and dimensional quality.

Benefits

  • High machining speed
  • Good dimensional accuracy
  • Low cost for large batches
  • Better use of raw material
  • High productivity

05

Turning, milling and mechanical fitting

Conventional machines for turning, milling and fitting (drilling, tapping and sanding) for non-serial production, maintenance parts, pre-machining for CNC and part finishing.

Technology

Drills, reamers and indexable tools chosen with suppliers that provide technical support and updates.

Equipment

  • 2 Cleaver engine lathes: 1,500 mm between centers, Ø 660 mm swing

  • Atlas 7/30 thread rolling machine, threads up to 30 mm

  • Manual milling machine

Benefits

  • Quick adjustments and maintenance work
  • Raw material in varied dimensions

06

Development and supply of assemblies

Besides machining and welding, we develop, assemble and supply technical assemblies based on the customer's product. With in-house processes and a chain of about 50 suppliers, we deliver an integrated solution: cutting, bending, injection, casting, finishing, treatment, assembly, machining and bonding.

Technology

We currently supply assemblies for tractor, combine and agricultural implement assembly lines, and technical assemblies for the machinery industry, such as hydraulic control levers.

Equipment

  • Assembly bench with pneumatic tools
  • 2 MIG welding machines
  • 2 brazing units
  • 1 spot welding machine

Benefits

  • Pre-assembled units, ready for the line
  • Assured quality
  • Fewer parts and suppliers for the customer to manage
