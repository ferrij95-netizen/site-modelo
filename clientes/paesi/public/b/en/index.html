What Paesi does

01

Turning with driven tools

With driven-tool CNC machines and an indexing chuck, we perform simultaneous precision turning and milling in a single process.

02

CNC milling

Serial and non-serial CNC milling of ferrous and non-ferrous materials, even after heat treatment.

03

CNC turning

Precision turned parts, serial or non-serial, in ferrous and non-ferrous materials.

04

Multi-slide CNC lathe (up to 60 mm)

Multi-slide CNC lathe with automatic bar feeding for series production.

05

Turning, milling and mechanical fitting

Conventional machines for turning, milling and fitting (drilling, tapping and sanding) for non-serial production, maintenance parts, pre-machining for CNC and part finishing..

06

Development and supply of assemblies

Besides machining and welding, we develop, assemble and supply technical assemblies based on the customer's product.

Traction shaft machining

Hydraulic drive shaft for tractors

Because it goes into a wide range of machines, the drive shaft has to be produced continuously and in large quantities.

Machining hardened D6 tool steel

The customer had quality and cost problems with the supply of vanes. Suppliers struggled to machine the material because of its hardness and strength, which drove up cycle time and tooling costs.
