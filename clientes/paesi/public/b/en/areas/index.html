Industries

Agricultural, petrochemical and machinery: three industries, one machine shop.

Agricultural equipment

The agricultural industry is always looking for technologies and processes that cut costs. We supply parts and assemblies for assembly lines and spare parts for tractors and agricultural implements.

We work with customers and suppliers on purchase planning and economical batch production to meet delivery dates. We keep in-house stock to serve customers.

Manufacturers needed pre-assembled units delivered straight to the line, without in-house manufacturing, assembly and inventory management. Paesi developed its method with customers and partnered with Brazilian and international suppliers to deliver large volumes: welded assemblies, mounted assemblies, transmissions and gears.

Products supplied

  • Hydraulic control assemblies
  • Fuel caps
  • Bearing housings
  • Dipsticks
  • Tie rods
  • Hydraulic assemblies
  • Tank mounting straps
  • Shims, shafts and bushings
Case studies in this industry

Petrochemical equipment

A fast-developing industry that is always seeking new equipment and technology. We supply machined parts and assemblies with the quality and safety this industry requires.

In one project, the customer brought a wide range of parts still under development. We worked together on parts and processes until the prototype was approved: development, machining and support in assembling the prototypes. The partnership delivered in the shortest time and at the lowest cost, without compromising quality.

Machinery and tools

We supply serial and non-serial parts, with or without heat and surface treatment. CNC machines and up-to-date cutting tools let us make simple to complex parts with tighter form and position tolerances.

For the machinery industry we deliver complex machined parts and welded, painted assemblies and sheet metal, with finishing, treatment and small sub-assemblies. We also machine cutting and bending tools, serial parts for many industries and tool maintenance parts.
