01 · Oil and Gas

Autonomous robots for inspection of classified areas, with Ex certification.

Routine inspection in areas with explosion risk, painting at height and tank inspection. The Tupã Ex and Guaraci robots were developed in partnership with Petrobras.

  • Tupã ExAutonomous inspection
  • MacuxiPainting
  • GuaraciTank inspection
02 · Mining

Teleoperated robots for inspection and transport in hazardous areas.

Remote thermal inspection, load transport in confined spaces and on stairs, away from operator exposure. Tupã was developed in partnership with Vale.

  • TupãThermal inspection
  • AnhangáTransport
  • ThorElectric vehicle
03 · Logistics

Autonomous AMR robots for internal material handling.

Jaguar AMR navigates on its own between stations, with lidar, 3D cameras and safety stops in line with NR12.

  • Jaguar AMRAutonomous transport
04 · Agriculture

Autonomous grain sample collection.

An autonomous arm for grain sampling and two projects approved in 2025, Jasuka and Nhandecy, aimed at small and medium-sized farms.

  • Sample CollectorAutonomous sampling
05 · Nuclear

Teleoperated robots for inspection of hazardous areas.

Remote inspection in environments where human presence must be avoided, with teleoperated robots, cameras and onboard sensors.

  • TupãThermal inspection
  • AnhangáTransport
06 · Health

Autonomous robots for disinfecting environments.

Jaci combines UV-C light and ozonated mist to disinfect rooms, operating theaters and areas at risk of infection.
