Services with robots.

Instor brings the robot and the team to your plant: inspection, painting and special projects, with a digital report at the end.

Service 01

Robotic boiler inspection

Boiler inspection with a robot and a digital record of every measured point, with no scaffolding and the team kept out of the confined space.

  • Thickness measurement and corrosion mapping
  • Georeferenced images and data for each point
  • Digital report at the end of the shutdown

Service 02

Tank inspection

Tank floor and shell inspection with the Angoerá scanner and the Guaraci hexapod, including an Ex version for classified areas.

  • Angoerá and Angoerá Ex scanner
  • Ex probe for classified areas
  • Onboard ultrasound for corrosion

Service 03

Robotic painting

The Macuxi robot applies paint and coatings to carbon steel surfaces at height, operated remotely by the Instor team.

  • Up to 200 m² per hour
  • Any paint or coating
  • Night work with LED lighting
