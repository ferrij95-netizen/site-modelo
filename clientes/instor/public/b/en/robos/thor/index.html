Thor

4x4 electric vehicle for carrying loads in restricted spaces.

A 100% electric utility vehicle with four-wheel steering: it moves sideways, turns on its own axis and gets into places where a regular vehicle cannot maneuver. It can carry a mini crane and be operated remotely.

  1. 01Four-wheel drive and steering
  2. 02Moves sideways or turns on its own axis
  3. 03Optional mini crane with electro-hydraulic outriggers
  4. 04Full remote control by joystick
  5. 05Cab suitable for people with reduced mobility
