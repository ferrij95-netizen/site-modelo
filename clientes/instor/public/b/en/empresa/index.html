In-house engineering since 2008.

Instor was founded by students from the UFRGS Physical Metallurgy Laboratory and today develops robots with Petrobras, Vale and Finep.

Mission

Reduce human exposure to high-risk situations and increase process efficiency through mobile robotics.

Vision

To be a reference in high-quality mobile robotics.

Values
  • Passion for what we doEvery robot is born from in-house engineering, from mechanical design to software.
  • Drive to overcome challengesProblems no one has solved yet are the starting point of our projects.
  • Thinking outside the boxFrom incremental to radical innovation, always grounded in the customer's operation.
Track record

Twenty-two years, one robot at a time.

  1. 2004

    The founders, still students, start at the Physical Metallurgy Laboratory (LAMEF) of the UFRGS School of Engineering.

  2. 2008

    Approved by the CEI/UFRGS incubator, Instor is founded in July after a technical and economic feasibility study.

  3. 2009

    Joins the Sebrae-RS program for the oil, gas and energy chain of Rio Grande do Sul.

  4. 2010

    Wins innovation funding in calls from Finep (Prime and Pre-Salt), Sebrae-RS, Senai and Fapergs.

  5. 2012

    Graduates from the incubator, moves to its own headquarters and becomes a registered Petrobras supplier.

  6. 2018

    Launches Macuxi, an industrial painting robot.

  7. 2019

    Develops Tupã, a thermal inspection robot, in partnership with Vale.

  8. 2020

    Enters Ex robotics with TUPAEX and launches Jaci, an autonomous germicidal robot.

  9. 2021

    Launches Jaguar AMR for the logistics sector.

  10. 2022

    Launches Tupã Ex, an Ex-certified tracked robot for classified areas, with Petrobras.

  11. 2023

    Develops Guaraci, a hexapod for tank inspection, with Petrobras.

  12. 2024

    Joins SPRINT Robotics and gets the Hexapod project approved in Finep's Radical Innovations for the Electric Sector program.

  13. 2025

    Becomes a member of Abendi and gets the Jasuka and Nhandecy agricultural projects approved.

  14. 2026

    Develops the Angoerá scanner, the Angoerá Ex and the Ex Probe for service work.
