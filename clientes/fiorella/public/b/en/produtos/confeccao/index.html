Interlinings for clothing

Fusible interlinings with stiff hand for a structured drape, and nonwoven care labels.

Stiff fusible interlinings made of nonwoven, giving the finished garment a structured drape and adding body to the fabric, with simplified manufacturing and fusing. Suitable for fusing onto fabrics for womenswear and menswear.
