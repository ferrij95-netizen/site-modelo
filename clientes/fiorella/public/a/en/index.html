Process

How a chemical bonding nonwoven is made

No spinning and no weaving: polyester and viscose fibers form a web that the resin bonds together. The result is a light, even material tuned to each use.

  1. 01

    Fibers

    Polyester and/or viscose, chosen for the hand and strength the application calls for.

  2. 02

    Web

    The fibers are opened and laid into a continuous, even web.

  3. 03

    Bonding

    The resin binds the fibers and sets stiffness, softness and how the interlining tears.

  4. 04

    Curing

    The web goes through the oven, where the resin cures and the nonwoven becomes stable.

  5. 05

    Roll

    Finishing, slitting to the widths ordered and winding, under ISO 9001 control.

About Fiorella

A nonwovens manufacturer with 49 years of history

Fiorella Brasil is a nonwovens manufacturer founded in 1977, producing chemical bonding nonwovens in polyester and/or viscose for a wide range of segments.

Through the use of technology, efficient processes and the great dedication of its entire team, it has come through these many years with great success.

It serves the entire Brazilian market and the Americas, with products specially developed for each specific purpose.
