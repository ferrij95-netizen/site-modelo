Products

Chemical bonding nonwovens in polyester and/or viscose for five fields. Below, every reference in the interlining range.

Embroidery backings

Easy-tear backings in every direction, with stiff, soft or extra-soft hand, plus double-sided interlining for appliqué.

See the range

Interlinings for clothing

Fusible interlinings with stiff hand for a structured drape, and nonwoven care labels.

See the range

Automotive industry

Raw material for vehicle protection and release materials for thermoformed parts. Adaptable, strong nonwovens with special characteristics for each application.

See the range

Filtration industry

Nonwovens specially developed for air and liquid filtration, with strength and permeability suited to each use.

See the range

Multiple applications

All the versatility of nonwovens for a variety of projects: absorbency, stiffness, strength, softness. Includes cleaning materials and the craft range.
