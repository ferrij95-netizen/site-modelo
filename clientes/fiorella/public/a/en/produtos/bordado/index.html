Embroidery backings

Easy-tear backings in every direction, with stiff, soft or extra-soft hand, plus double-sided interlining for appliqué.

The backing supports the embroidery while the needles pierce the fabric and gives it body throughout the life of the garment. Even though they tear easily in every direction, they are strong backings, with weights that vary by reference.

  • Stiff hand · easy tear
  • Soft hand · easy tear
  • Extra soft · trim with scissors
  • Double-sided · laser cut
