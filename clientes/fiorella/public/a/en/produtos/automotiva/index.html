Automotive industry

Raw material for vehicle protection and release materials for thermoformed parts. Adaptable, strong nonwovens with special characteristics for each application.

For the automotive industry, Fiorella produces raw material for vehicle protection and release materials for thermoformed parts: adaptable, strong nonwovens with special characteristics defined for each application.

  • Chemical bonding nonwovenspolyester and/or viscose
  • +18 millionmeters of nonwovens produced every year
  • ISO 9001:2015SGS · UKAS

What to include in your request

  • Your application and process (thermoforming, air or liquid filtration...)
  • Composition: polyester, viscose or blend
  • Target weight (g/m²)
  • Roll width and length
  • Properties: stiffness, softness, strength, permeability, absorbency
  • Estimated monthly volume
