Who we are

Not just a part. A better solution for your company.

Founded in the 1990s and headquartered in Williamsport, Pennsylvania, PMT Machining specializes in manual and CNC machined parts for original equipment manufacturers and industrial manufacturers of all sizes.

Real quality begins with focusing on each customer's individual needs, then finding solutions to meet them efficiently. Our sister company, PMT Industrial, supplies power transmission, motors, drives and pneumatics, so one call can cover the part and what drives it.
