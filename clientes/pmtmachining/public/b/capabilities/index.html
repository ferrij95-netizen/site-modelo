Capabilities

Machining and design

From concept, to prototype, to manufacture.

Years of hands-on knowledge in industrial manufacturing let us help your company from concept to prototype to production. Our equipment and experience let us manufacture in the most efficient way possible, whether the job is large or small.

01

CNC milling and turning

A wide range of high-precision CNC lathes and mills, with secondary and finishing equipment, for parts of all shapes and sizes.

  • CNC mill
  • CNC lathe
  • Precision grinding
02

OEM replacement parts

Worn or obsolete part on a machine you depend on? We make OEM replacement components, from gears and bearing hubs to specialty shafts, and you can save money by switching your machining to us.

  • Gears and sprockets
  • Bearing hubs
  • Specialty shafts
03

Plastic and non-metal parts

Plastic gears, wear parts and guides machined from engineering plastics and phenolic, along with aluminum, steel and bronze.

  • Plastic gears
  • Phenolic
  • Wear parts and guides
04

Custom machining, fabrication and repair

General and custom machining, prototypes, fabrication and repair work, handled by an experienced staff that can take on both small and large requests.

  • Prototypes
  • Fabrication and repair
  • General machining
05

Industrial distribution

PMT Industrial, Inc. distributes power transmission, electric motors and drives, and pneumatic products. Our goal is to apply, not just supply, the right products for your company.

  • Power transmission
  • Motors and drives
  • Pneumatics
