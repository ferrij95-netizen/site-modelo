About us

A Williamsport machine shop

PMT Machining was founded in the 1990s in Williamsport, Pennsylvania, to make manual and CNC machined parts for original equipment manufacturers and industrial manufacturers of all sizes.

Our production facility has a wide range of high-precision lathes and mills, secondary equipment and finishing machines, backed by an experienced staff. We make plastic gears, bearing hubs, specialty shafts and much more.
