How we work

Your new website first. The project conversation after.

We study your company and your current website and build two complete versions of the new site, with your logo, your colors, photos of your shop and the parts you make. Both go live on a preview link.

  1. 01

    Study

    We review your company, your current website, your capabilities and how buyers find you today.

    What you getA 30-minute call and access to the material you already have.

  2. 02

    Two versions live

    We design two complete versions of your new website and publish each one on a preview link.

    What you getLinks to open on your computer and your phone.

  3. 03

    Choice and changes

    You pick a version, ask for changes and approve each page.

    What you getA change list tracked until approval.

  4. 04

    Launch

    The website goes live on your domain, with the RFQ form, Google search setup and contact tracking.

    What you getRFQs arriving in the right inbox.

  5. 05

    Ongoing care

    We update capabilities and parts, run your ads and organize the sales routine.

    What you getChange requests by message and an RFQ report.
