How we work

First the finished website. Then the conversation about the project.

We study your company and your current website and build two complete versions of the new site, with your logo, colors, plant photos and products. Both go live on a preview link.

  1. 01

    Research

    We look at your company, current website, product lines and how buyers find you.

    What you getA 30-minute call and access to the material you already have.

  2. 02

    Two versions live

    We design two complete versions of the new site and publish each one on a preview link.

    What you getLinks to open on desktop and mobile.

  3. 03

    Your pick and revisions

    You choose a version, request changes and approve each page.

    What you getA revision list tracked until approval.

  4. 04

    Launch

    The site on your domain, with catalog, languages, Google search setup and lead tracking.

    What you getQuote requests landing in the right inbox.

  5. 05

    Ongoing support

    We update the catalog, run your ads and organize your sales routine.

    What you getChange requests handled by message, plus a quote report.
