What usually holds sales back

Your shop is capable. Your website does not show it yet.

  • A website that hides the shop

    Buyers cannot see your machines, tolerances, materials or part envelope, so they compare you with any other shop on price alone.

  • RFQs that arrive incomplete

    Quotes come in by email and phone without drawings, quantities or material, and your estimator spends the first call asking for them.

  • Certifications nobody finds

    ISO 9001, AS9100 or ITAR registration are buried in a PDF or missing, and buyers filling their supplier list move on.

  • Built for a desktop from years ago

    Engineers and buyers research on their phones, and the site breaks, loads slowly or looks like the company stopped investing.

    FAQ

    What manufacturers usually ask.

    All questions
    Can you work with a shop in the United States?

    Yes. Everything happens online: a video call to understand your shop, live preview links to review the two versions, and change requests by email or message.

    Do you keep our logo and colors?

    Yes. The new website starts from your current identity: logo, colors, photos and the parts you make. If buyers know your current site structure, we keep it and make it better.

    Can buyers upload drawings with the RFQ?

    Yes. The form takes STEP, IGES, DXF and PDF files along with quantities, material, finish and due date. For controlled data, the form can collect only the contact.
