Industries

Every industry buys differently.

A medical device engineer looks for different things than an aerospace buyer. The website shows first what weighs most in each decision.

01

CNC machine shops

What the buyer looks for

Machine capacity, tolerances, materials and lead time, from prototype to production runs.

What the website shows
  • Equipment list
  • Parts you have made
  • Materials and tolerances
  • RFQ with drawing upload
02

Aerospace and defense suppliers

What the buyer looks for

AS9100, ITAR registration, first article inspection and full traceability.

What the website shows
  • Quality system page
  • Certifications up front
  • Inspection equipment
  • Controlled-data RFQ option
03

Medical device manufacturing

What the buyer looks for

ISO 13485, clean processes, documentation and validated production.

What the website shows
  • Certifications and processes
  • Materials for medical parts
  • Documentation and traceability
  • Confidential RFQ
04

Fabrication and welding

What the buyer looks for

Welding certifications, sheet and plate capacity, finishing and assembly.

What the website shows
  • Cutting, forming and welding
  • Certified welders
  • Finishing and assembly
  • Project gallery
05

Plastics and molding

What the buyer looks for

Materials, press tonnage, tooling, secondary operations and volumes.

What the website shows
  • Presses and tonnage
  • Resins and materials
  • Tooling and mold care
  • Sample request
06

Industrial equipment makers

What the buyer looks for

Models, specifications, service and spare parts, plus dealers by region.

What the website shows
  • A page per machine
  • Videos of the machines running
  • Service and spare parts
  • Dealers and reps
