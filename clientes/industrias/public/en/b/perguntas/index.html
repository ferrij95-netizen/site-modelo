FAQ

What manufacturers usually ask.

Can you work with a shop in the United States?

Yes. Everything happens online: a video call to understand your shop, live preview links to review the two versions, and change requests by email or message.

Do you keep our logo and colors?

Yes. The new website starts from your current identity: logo, colors, photos and the parts you make. If buyers know your current site structure, we keep it and make it better.

Can buyers upload drawings with the RFQ?

Yes. The form takes STEP, IGES, DXF and PDF files along with quantities, material, finish and due date. For controlled data, the form can collect only the contact.

Do we need new photos of the shop?

We use the photos you already have. If something is missing, we tell you what is worth photographing and mark where an image is temporary.

Who receives the RFQs?

Whoever you choose: your estimator’s email, a sales inbox or the CRM. Each request arrives with the drawing, the quantities and the buyer’s details.

Can you also build a Spanish version?

Yes, with reviewed technical copy and a language switch on every page.

What happens after launch?

We keep taking care of it: new capabilities, machines and parts, new pages, ads and the sales routine. Change requests are sent by message.
