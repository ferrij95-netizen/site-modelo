RFQ and catalog

Buyers send a complete RFQ the first time.

Your estimator gets the drawing, the quantity and the material in the first message, and buyers of standard parts build a quote list in a few clicks.

01

RFQ with drawing upload

Buyers attach STEP, IGES, DXF or PDF files and fill in quantities, material, finish and due date in one form.

  • STEP, IGES, DXF and PDF files
  • Quantities, material and finish
  • Due date and company details
02

Catalog and quote list

Standard parts with search, filters and spec sheets, where the buyer builds a list and sends it as one quote request.

  • Search by part number and material
  • Spec sheet on every part
  • One request for the whole list
03

Controlled data

If your shop handles ITAR or export-controlled data, the form can collect only the contact and leave drawings to your own secure transfer.

  • Contact-only RFQ option
  • Link to your secure file transfer
  • Clear notice for buyers
