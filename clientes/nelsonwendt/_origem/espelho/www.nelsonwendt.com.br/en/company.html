Our history

We have always been a family business. Nelson Wendt Alimentos started the rice processing in 1964, aiming to deliver a great product to the consumers. The rice we produce fills the plates of thousands of people every day. We believe in real care. As in a family. That is something that is present in all stages of our production.

1964

At just 21 years old, young Nelson Wendt, together with his father, Alfredo Wendt, began their production in a small mill in the city of Pelotas, RS.

1978

Beginning of parboiled rice production.

1988

Construction of the company's first branch in Pelotas/RS. With the new facility, we tripled our parboiled rice production capacity.

1989

The Emoções brand is born to serve the cities of Recife (PE) and Maceió (AL) with quality and care as a priority. Soon, Arroz Emoções won over the Northeastern market and became a beloved brand in the region.

2001

Inauguration of the first industrial headquarters in the Brazilian Northeast, in Recife (PE).

2011

Beginning of parboiled rice exports to the markets of South, Central and North America; Africa and Europe.

2017

Implementation of the renewable energy project in the industries of Pelotas, ensuring certification from the Brazilian GHG Protocol Program.

2023

We earned the HACCP seal, which internationally certifies the company's commitment to promoting the distribution of safe food.

2024

We celebrate 60 years of Nelson Wendt.

2025

Arroz Emoções wins 1st place for the 10th consecutive year in consumption and preference in the Marcas Que Eu Gosto survey by the newspaper Folha de Pernambuco. Since 2006, it has been recognized as the most beloved brand by consumers in Recife (PE) and Salvador (BA).
