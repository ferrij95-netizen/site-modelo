Our history

We have always been a family business. Nelson Wendt Alimentos started the rice processing in 1964, aiming to deliver a great product to the consumers. The rice we produce fills the plates of thousands of people every day. We believe in real care. As in a family. That is something that is present in all stages of our production.

International distribution

After over 10 years of experience in exports, selling our rice to South, Central and North America, Africa, Europe and the Middle East, we are able to serve our international clients in the most diverse ways, always with a high quality product, HACCP Certified.

A chain of care

We are proud to be involved in 100% of the chain of the rice we export: since its harvest in southern Brazil, through processing, packaging and distribution. All with great carefulness, to guarantee a safe and very tasty product, as our clients deserve.
