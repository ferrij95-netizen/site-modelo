About us

From a garage to the Olympic Torch

H & H Machined Products was founded in 1980 by Robert Heinlein and William Hassell, hence the name H & H. Starting with a couple of Brown & Sharpe screw machines and a 1907 Lodge & Shipley engine lathe, Bob and Bill grew the company from a garage operation into our current 10,500 square foot facility, adding CNC turning, automatic screw machines and CNC milling.

In July 1995 we worked with American Meter Company of Erie to machine parts for the Olympic Torches of the 1996 Atlanta Games. We made the mechanical parts inside the torch and helped redesign its two gold collars. To this day our slogan remains “Lighting the flame of quality and excellence worldwide.” In 2009 Andrew and Debra Foyle acquired the company, committed to the same quality and excellence.
