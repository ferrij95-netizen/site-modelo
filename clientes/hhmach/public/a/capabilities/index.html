Capabilities

Capabilities

A greater range of metal machining services than you will find from any single machining source.

Our cellular operations make us more cost effective. Each highly trained technician sets tooling, runs operations and sets second and third machines at the same time, while secondary operations such as sanding, chamfering and deburring happen right at the work station. No wasted time, no wasted people and no wasted money, a saving we pass along to you.

  1. 01

    Swiss turning

    Long, slender pins, shafts and connector pins held to tight tolerances, turned complete on Swiss-type machines.

    • Pins and shafts
    • Connector pins
    • Small diameters from .125 in.
  2. 02

    CNC turning and milling

    Precision CNC production turning lathes, Mazak machining center and Haas CNC mill for flanges, rings, bodies and parts up to 6 in. and more.

    • CNC production lathes
    • Mazak machining center
    • Haas CNC milling
  3. 03

    Automatic screw machining

    The Brown & Sharpe screw machines we started with have grown into a high-volume department for threaded parts, fittings and spacers.

    • Threaded parts
    • Fittings and spacers
    • Washers
  4. 04

    Sub-assemblies

    Connector sub-assemblies and multi-part components assembled in house, so you receive a part ready for your line.

    • Connector sub-assemblies
    • Hardware kits
    • Knurling and secondary ops
  5. 05

    Kanban and JIT stocking

    Our goal is 100% on-time delivery with every order. We stock parts for customers who use Kanban or other inventory control systems, so you are never kept waiting for a crucial part.

    • Kanban parts stocking
    • Just-in-time delivery
    • 100% on-time goal
