Our company

In an increasingly demanding market, Reval Serras seeks to stand out through actions that prioritize quality and precision in its products and services. The company manufactures saws for cutting solid wood, panels, aluminum, plastic, acrylic, among others. In the Reval Laser subdivision, laser cutting and bending services are offered.

Our history

Founded in 1986, Reval has produced circular saw blades for cutting wood, aluminium and steel and band saw blades for wood, serving the national and international markets. Headquartered in Caxias do Sul, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, the company stands out in circular saw blade production in both the commercial and industrial lines.

With a policy of constantly updating its professionals, equipment, processes and projects, Reval guarantees its clients products of high technology, precision and durability.

  1. Mission

    The mission of Reval is to offer high-quality products and services in its field of operation. The company strives for constant improvement, investing in modern technology and effective work methods to ensure the continuous satisfaction of its customers.
