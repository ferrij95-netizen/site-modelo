Our history

We uphold a tradition of excellence

Founded in 1986, Reval has produced circular saw blades for cutting wood, aluminium and steel and band saw blades for wood, serving the national and international markets. Headquartered in Caxias do Sul, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, the company stands out in circular saw blade production in both the commercial and industrial lines.

With a policy of constantly updating its professionals, equipment, processes and projects, Reval guarantees its clients products of high technology, precision and durability.
