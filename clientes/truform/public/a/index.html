Who we are

Critical parts, machined by people who take pride in them.

With over 30 years of experience supplying customers from industrial to aerospace, TruForm Machine is an AS9100 and customer award-winning CNC supplier. From raw material to metal finish, we are your “part complete” source.

Our parts are machined on state-of-the-art Okuma CNC production equipment by experienced operators, aided by highly skilled programmers. We maintain the highest level of quality and delivery, because our customers rely on it, and we expect it.

All capabilities
  1. 01

    Precision CNC turning

    Okuma Captain and LT200 multi-axis lathes with live tooling, dual spindles and dual turrets, running bar up to 3 1/2 in. for parts complete in one setup, including hard turning.

    • Bar capacity up to 3 1/2 in.
    • 7-axis dual spindle, live tooling
    • Hard turning
  2. 02

    Critical aerospace components

    High-precision machining in all types of metals, including exotic alloys, with close dimensional and concentricity requirements, critical finishes, and burr-free corners, seats and threads.

    • Close concentricity
    • Critical finish requirements
    • Burr-free seats and threads
  3. 03

    Conditioning and finishing

    We manage heat treat, plating, coating and marking so you receive a finished part, packaged to your specification.

    • Heat treat
    • Cad and silver plate
    • Black oxide and anodize
    • Passivate, Teflon, dry film
    • Laser and dot peen marking
    • Magnetic particle inspection
