About us

Details count

TruForm Machine has supplied precision machined components from Jackson, Michigan for more than 30 years, from industrial work to the aerospace parts that earned us three Eaton Supplier Excellence Awards.

Our team approach and commitment to continuous improvement make us a leading supplier in a world of “faster, better and cheaper.” Our employees are involved in root cause and long-term corrective action, and they take pride in their work and the company they work for.
