Quality

Measured, logged, certified

Every heat treatment carried out by Thermotech is monitored. The parts ship with a quality certificate together with the graphic curve of the procedure performed.

Calibration and monitoring

All equipment is calibrated by RBC-traceable laboratories every six or twelve months, or as requested by the customer.

To assure material quality, Thermotech runs its own quality laboratory with 3 hardness testers (King, portable and bench), so we can measure small and large parts anywhere, plus 1 metallographic microscope and 1 metallographic polisher.

Laboratory

  • Portable hardness tester

    Measures hardness on the part itself, including large parts and on site.

  • Bench hardness tester

    Rockwell hardness tests on specimens and smaller parts.

  • Metallographic microscope

    Checks the microstructure obtained after treatment.

Plus a King hardness tester and a metallographic polisher to prepare samples.

Quality Policy

Thermotech’s management, together with its employees, sets as its Quality Policy the commitment to provide heat treatment and repair services for steel and cast iron parts, continuously focusing on:

  • The satisfaction of its customers
  • Improving its Quality Management System
  • Meeting the applicable requirements of interested parties
  • Achieving a sustainable financial return
