Services

Heat treatment

Heat treatments are a set of operations designed to change the properties of steels and other materials through heating and cooling under controlled conditions.

Our 1,300 m² plant has a production capacity of 500 t/month, with 4 bell-type furnaces and 8 load-building bases, so we can run closed loads from 700 kg up to 10,000 kg. The furnaces are LPG-fired and treat loads at temperatures of up to 1,200 °C.

Treatments performed

Normalizing

Refines and evens out the structure of cast or forged steel, leaving the part ready for machining or the next step.

Typical range: 850 – 950 °CCooling: in air

Tempering

Done after hardening: relieves brittleness and sets the final hardness for the part’s application.

Typical range: 150 – 650 °CCooling: in air

Annealing

Heating followed by slow cooling inside the furnace, to soften the material and make machining easier.

Typical range: 800 – 900 °CCooling: slow, in furnace

Stress Relief

Removes stresses left by casting, welding or machining, without changing the part’s structure.

Typical range: 550 – 650 °CCooling: slow, in furnace

Quench & Temper

Hardening followed by tempering: combines strength and toughness in critical parts.

Typical range: hardening + temperingCooling: fast (quench)

Solution Annealing

High heat and fast cooling for stainless and manganese steels, restoring toughness and corrosion resistance.

Typical range: 1,050 – 1,150 °CCooling: fast (quench)

Ferritizing

For cast iron: turns the matrix into ferrite, for more ductility and better machinability.

Typical range: 700 – 760 °CCooling: slow, in furnace

Pearlitizing

For cast iron: forms a pearlitic matrix, for higher strength and wear resistance.

Typical range: 880 – 920 °CCooling: in air

Hardening

Heating to austenitizing temperature and rapid cooling, to reach high hardness.

Typical range: 800 – 900 °CCooling: fast (quench)

Spheroidizing

Long soak just below the critical range: leaves the steel soft and highly formable.

Typical range: 680 – 720 °CCooling: slow, in furnace

Reference ranges from the literature. Each part’s procedure is set by Thermotech’s engineering team.

How we work

  1. 1

    Review

    Engineers set the procedure for the material and the required property.

  2. 2

    Load building

    Parts are built up on one of 8 bases, from 700 kg to 10 t.

  3. 3

    Bell furnace

    The furnace is lowered over the base and fires on LPG, up to 1,200 °C.

  4. 4

    Monitored cycle

    Temperature logged from start to finish, with a graphic curve.

  5. 5

    Laboratory

    Hardness and microstructure checked in our own lab.

  6. 6

    Certificate & delivery

    Parts ship with a quality certificate and the cycle curve.
