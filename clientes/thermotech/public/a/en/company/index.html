Company

Founded on October 1, 2009, Thermotech provides heat treatment services to a wide range of sectors: mechanical industry, mining, crushing, valves, pumps, automotive, railway, agricultural and more.

Our 1,300 m² plant has a production capacity of 500 t/month, with 4 bell-type furnaces and 8 load-building bases, so we can run closed loads from 700 kg up to 10,000 kg. The furnaces are LPG-fired and treat loads at temperatures of up to 1,200 °C.

Our team of respected metallurgy specialists includes a chemical engineer, a metallurgical engineer and a metallurgy technician, who help develop the best procedure for each job and so achieve the best mechanical properties after heat treatment.
