A little bit about PPM

Performance Pattern & Machine first started in 1980 by Vince Herman and George Munger. Its beginning as a pattern shop has developed over the years into the multi-faceted business it is today.

Over time, we have expanded our family business in order to provide more complete services by keeping up with the technological advances in the industry. Today, we have technologically advanced equipment manned by an experienced staff.

We have earned our reputation through hard work, uncompromising precision and constant learning and adapting. We love what we do, and the variety of projects we accomplish proves the scope of our capabilities.
