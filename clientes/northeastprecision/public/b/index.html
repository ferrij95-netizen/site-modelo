How a job moves

From drawing to shipment

  1. 01

    Quote

    Send your drawing, annual volume and material. We quote steel and aluminum runs from 100 to 25,000 pieces.

  2. 02

    Plan & track

    Every order is tracked in JobBOSS from quotation to shipment, with 15-year record retention.

  3. 03

    Machine 24/7

    Mori Seiki, Brother Speedio and Okuma centers, tended day and night by Universal Robots.

  4. 04

    Inspect

    Fowler CMM, Keyence LM-X and OGP vision systems, with FAI, APQP and PPAP documentation in High QA.

  5. 05

    Wash & ship

    Ramco ultrasonic parts washer with deionized water option, then out the door on time.
