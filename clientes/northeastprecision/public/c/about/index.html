Who we are

A trusted partner for a wide range of industries

Since our founding in 1985, North East Precision CNC has become a leader in precision manufacturing, driven by the need for high-quality parts with reliable on-time delivery. Over the years, we have built a reputation for innovation and dependability, making us a trusted partner for a wide range of industries.

We have 18 machines, including lathes and mills, with eight robots on site, and we utilize AI and other new technologies daily. Our shop is ahead of its time for its size, with 20 full-time employees, family-run, and in business for 40 years.

Expertise and Technology

  • Aluminum, Stainless Steel, and Steels Machining.
  • Automation Centers with Universal Robots: Enhancing efficiency, consistency and precision in our CNC milling and lathe departments.

Commitment to Quality

Quality and customer service are at the heart of North East Precision CNC. Our fully-equipped quality control department ensures that only the best parts, products, and components leave our shop. We are dedicated to continuous improvement through the ISO 9001 process, and our recent investments in state-of-the-art machines, advanced inspection methods and automation further enhance our capabilities.

Solar & environment concerns

Minimizing our carbon footprint

We keep our impact on our environment at the forefront of our mind as we strive to minimize our carbon footprint. To that end, we have installed 365 solar panels boasting a 137 KW array.

North East Precision CNC is recycling 100% of all our cutting chips and fluids, achieving a zero-waste stream.
