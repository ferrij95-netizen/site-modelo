Aluminum Machining

Aluminum Machining: Precision, Efficiency, and Versatility in CNC Manufacturing

At North East Precision CNC, aluminum machining is one of our specialties. As a go-to material in industries like aerospace, automotive, medical, and consumer products, aluminum offers a powerful combination of strength, machinability, and lightweight performance.

What is aluminum machining?

Complex geometries, tight tolerances

Aluminum machining uses CNC machines to shape, drill, mill, or cut aluminum into precise parts or components. Thanks to its strength-to-weight ratio, corrosion resistance, and thermal conductivity, it is a top choice for high-performance applications. We utilize advanced multi-axis CNC mills, lathes, and machining centers to produce complex aluminum parts with exceptional accuracy and consistency.

Why aluminum

Why choose aluminum for CNC machining

01

Excellent Machinability

Aluminum is easier to machine than many other metals, which allows us to:

  • Cut faster and reduce cycle times
  • Achieve tight tolerances and clean finishes
  • Extend tool life and lower machining costs
02

High Strength-to-Weight Ratio

Structural integrity without excess weight, ideal for:

  • Aerospace components
  • Automotive parts
  • Portable consumer products
03

Corrosion Resistance and Thermal Conductivity

A protective oxide layer resists corrosion and heat dissipates efficiently, ideal for:

  • Heat sinks
  • Engine housings
  • Electronic enclosures

Our aluminum machining capabilities

  • 3-, 4-, and 5-axis CNC milling
  • CNC turning for round components
  • Drilling, tapping, and threading
  • Surface finishing such as anodizing, bead blasting, and powder coating

Aluminum at high volumes

  • High-speed spindles that optimize cutting efficiency
  • Automated material handling for uninterrupted production
  • Real-time quality monitoring to ensure part consistency
