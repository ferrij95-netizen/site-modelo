Software

Quality and traceability, from quote to shipment

High QA, uniPoint and JobBOSS connect engineering, production and inspection.

High QA®

High QA software unites engineering, production and inspection teams internally and throughout the supply chain with an integrated solution to plan, manage, document and control manufacturing quality from ballooning and planning to production, reporting and submissions. With this software, North East Precision CNC can meet customer needs for FAI, APQP and PPAP process requirements. We can generate quality documents for ISO 9001:2015.

uniPoint®

uniPoint is a complete compliance management software (QMS) solution that helps companies automate their processes.

JobBoss®

JobBOSS is a management software and manufacturing ERP system which allows us to monitor the status of a job from start to finish, ensuring comprehensive tracking and traceability of materials throughout the manufacturing process.

Job tracking

JobBoss Tracking System

North East Precision CNC uses a cutting-edge job tracking system called “JobBOSS”. JobBOSS enables us to track the status of a Customer Order from the initial quotation to the final shipment. This system provides visibility by tracking and tracing material throughout the entire manufacturing process. Record Retention is 15 years.
