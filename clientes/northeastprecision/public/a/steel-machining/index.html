Steel Machining

Steel Machining: Strength, Precision, and Durability for Demanding Applications

At North East Precision CNC, steel machining is at the core of what we do. From structural components to high-performance machine parts, steel is one of the most versatile and widely used materials in CNC manufacturing. With its excellent strength, durability, and adaptability, steel remains a top choice across industries like aerospace, defense, heavy equipment, energy, and industrial automation.

What is steel machining?

Complex geometries, tight tolerances

Steel machining refers to the process of shaping steel workpieces into finished parts using CNC equipment such as mills, lathes, and turning centers. We machine a wide range of steel grades, both ferrous and stainless, to meet the exact requirements of each part or project. Our multi-axis CNC machines, skilled technicians, and advanced programming allow us to produce complex geometries with tight tolerances and superior surface finishes.

Why steel

Why choose steel for CNC machining

01

Superior Strength and Toughness

Ideal for components that need to withstand high loads, impacts, or wear.

  • Gears and shafts
  • Structural brackets
  • High-pressure fittings
  • Machine bases and housings
02

Heat Treatment Compatibility

Steel responds well to heat treating, which can be used to:

  • Increase hardness
  • Improve wear resistance
  • Enhance tensile strength

Our steel machining capabilities

  • High-speed milling and turning for precision parts
  • Threading, boring, and tapping operations
  • Custom fixture design for complex steel parts
  • Post-machining processes, including heat treatment and surface finishing
  • In-house quality control with CMMs and optical comparators

Steel at high volumes

  • High-rigidity CNC machines capable of cutting hardened steels
  • Carbide tooling and optimized tool paths for efficiency and tool life
  • Coolant systems that reduce heat and prolong tool performance
  • Lights-out machining for scalable, around-the-clock production

Whether you need a single prototype or thousands of production parts, we tailor our machining process to your project’s needs.
