Available position

CNC Mill Machinist

Join our innovative team at North East Precision CNC! We are excited to offer opportunities for experienced, full-time CNC Machinists and Machine Operators for our 1st shift operations, with prior work experience and/or formal training on multi-axis lathes. Enjoy our state-of-the-art, climate-controlled workspace and have every Friday off.

Key responsibilities include operating and setting up CNC lathes that have robots attending them at times. Ability to demonstrate strong technical, organizational, and communication skills. You will work collaboratively in a clean and hazard-free environment, taking proper care of equipment and materials.

We have 18 machines, including lathes and mills, with eight robots on site, and we utilize AI and other new technologies daily. Our shop is ahead of its time for its size, with 20 full-time employees, family-run, and in business for 40 years.

Requirements

  • Experience or a strong interest in manufacturing high-precision metal products
  • Understanding of blueprints
  • US citizenship is required
  • A basic understanding of GD&T and ISO is a plus
  • Consistently lifting 40 pounds; mathematical skills
  • High school diploma or GED
  • Use of basic hand tools, measuring tools, height gauges, and comparators

Benefits

  • Every Friday off
  • Competitive wages
  • Medical insurance
  • Company matching 401(k)
  • Company-paid life insurance
  • Company-paid short and long-term disability
  • Vacation / sick pay
  • Holiday pay
  • Dental and vision at very low cost
