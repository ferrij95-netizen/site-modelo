About us

Family-run, ahead of its time for its size

With 40 years of experience, North East Precision CNC provides exceptional service to a wide range of industries. Our recent investments in CNC milling, lathe departments, state-of-the-art inspection equipment, and automation with Universal Robots enable us to meet the complex and evolving needs of our clients. Our fully climate-controlled, 14,600-square-foot facility operates 24/7, specializing in high production runs.
