News

Investing in our shop, year after year

Robots, inspection equipment, expansions and certifications since 2016.

  1. 2026

    Medical Devices: ISO 13485 in progress

    Coming soon: Medical Devices. ISO 13485:2016 Quality Management System implementation in progress.

  2. 2025

    Facility Updates

    Added 4 more Universal Robots and a 1,500 sq ft climate-controlled facility addition.

  3. 2024

    Automation

    The automation improvements added 4 more Universal Robots and a Keyence TM-X5000 Measurement System.

  4. 2023

    FFL Type 7 Manufacturing License

    North East Precision CNC has received its FFL Type 7 Federal License.

  5. 2023

    Quality Control

    The Quality Control improvements added a Keyence TM-X5000 Measurement System, a Fowler CMM Mark 1 Measuring System and our fifth Universal Robot.

  6. 2022

    Robot Automation

    Our fourth Universal Robot was purchased with the intention of moving from work center to work center for loading and unloading parts and doing deburring tasks as well.

  7. 2021

    Robot Automation

    North East Precision CNC put in its second and third Universal Robots with automated loading system.

  8. 2021

    Shop Air Conditioner

    Shop-wide air conditioning and air filtration system online summer of 2022.

  9. 2020

    Robot Automation

    In January 2020 North East Precision CNC put in its first automated machine loading system. The Enduro-Loader Pro paired with the Universal Robot allows us to load 5-axis parts day and night and increase capacity. The automation system is the first in our Industry 4.0 mindset.

    See our robot in action
  10. 2020

    Shipping and Parts Cleaning

    In early 2020 we added a custom Ramco parts washing unit and remodeled our shipping area. The machine comes equipped with an ultrasonic tank and an air knife drying unit, and can wash with deionized water for the customers that require it.

  11. 2019

    Clean Energy

    In the fall of 2019 North East Precision finished construction of its nearly 350-panel solar array. The system provides enough clean energy to power the entire facility and more.

  12. 2019

    High Speed Inspection

    In early 2019 North East Precision added a Sylvac F60T and new inspection station. The Sylvac F60T offers high speed inspection for even the most complicated of turned parts, measuring some in just seconds.

  13. 2018

    ISO 9001:2015 Certified!

    For over 30 years North East Precision CNC has been providing the highest quality machining services across many industries on a wide variety of materials. Our mission has always been to not only meet our customer’s expectations, but to exceed them. NEP CNC has achieved ISO 9001:2015 Certification.

  14. 2017

    Shop Expansion!

    Since 2016 we have gone from 5,000 square feet to a total of 10,000 square feet. This expansion has allowed us to add many more machines and to streamline our operation to be much more efficient.

  15. 2017

    New to our QC Department: Smart Scope

    The OGP Smart Scope Flash 302 is a versatile bench-top multi-sensor measurement machine. It combines optical, laser and tactile sensors to measure parts completely and efficiently.

  16. 2016

    DMU 50 Ecoline Milling Center

    Powerful technology with five-sided machining, variable table options from fixed to swivel rotary for 5-axis simultaneous machining, a 12,000 rpm motor spindle and a 30-pocket tool magazine.
