Capabilities

Precision machining and fabrication

Advanced multi-axis CNC machinery and technically competent people for your difficult machine work.

Whether your work involves importing your files to our CAD system, tool and gage fabrication, custom machines, process development or prototype to production, our people are ready to provide the expertise.

  1. 01

    Exotic material piece parts

    High-precision parts in the materials other shops avoid, including space flight materials. Research and development parts are welcome.

    • Space flight materials
    • Refractory and high-temperature alloys
    • Alloy and stainless steels
    • Ceramics and composites
  2. 02

    Special machinery and assemblies

    Machine fabrication and custom assemblies, built and fitted in our shop, with process development when the job needs a new method.

    • Machine fabrication
    • Custom assemblies
    • Process development
  3. 03

    Gages and fixtures

    Tool, gage and fixture fabrication with jig grinding and CMM inspection, so your parts are checked against tools you can trust.

    • Gage fabrication
    • Fixture fabrication
    • Jig and tool grinding
  4. 04

    Prototype to production

    From a single prototype to small production runs and general production support. We can usually turn your parts around in a few days or weeks, depending on quantity and difficulty.

    • Prototyping
    • Small production runs
    • General production support
