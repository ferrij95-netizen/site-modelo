About us

From the Mound site to Dayton

In 1993 the workers of the government facility in Miamisburg, Ohio were told the site would begin closing in 1994. In November 1994, Mound Manufacturing Center, Inc. became a reality for displaced workers of EG&G and DOE, the first business to go private from the Mound site.

We leased a building on the old Mound site from the city of Miamisburg for several years, growing our specialty in precision machining. In September 2006 we moved to Dayton, to the building we occupy today, with room to grow.
