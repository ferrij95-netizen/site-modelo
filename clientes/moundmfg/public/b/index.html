Who we are

Born at the Mound site. Built for parts that must be perfect.

Mound Manufacturing Center, Inc. (MMC) was founded in 1994 by the machinists of the Mound government facility in Miamisburg, Ohio, the first business to go private from the site. We kept the standards and brought them to commercial customers.

We are a hands-on shop: customers are welcome to visit and see how their parts are made and how they are progressing, from start to finish. We will sit down and discuss your parts from beginning to end, and always strive to give you the best quality and price for your money.
