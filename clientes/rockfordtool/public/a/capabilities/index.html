Capabilities

Capabilities

  1. 01

    Die engineering and construction

    Design with AutoCAD, DIEMAKER and Solid Edge for 2D or 3D drawings, and every step from concept and review to tool build, assembly, debug and production run. Properly designed dies that make consistent, quality parts.

    • In-house designers for rush jobs
    • Engineering changes
    • Reverse engineering
    • Die repair
    • Die tryout up to 250 tons
  2. 02

    CNC machining

    Five late-model vertical machining centers, including 5 axis DMG DMU 50s, and a CNC lathe with 17 in swing, programmed in Mastercam 3D. Parts to customer specifications and components for new tooling and repairs, in small or large quantities.

    • Table travel to 20 × 50 in
    • 10,000 RPM spindle
    • 5 axis DMG DMU 50
    • Mastercam 3D
  3. 03

    Wire EDM

    Five late-model, high-speed automated EDM machines for tightly toleranced parts, programmed with DP Technology Esprit. Used for our dies and for customer contract work.

    • Automatic threading
    • Large travel, submerged tanks
    • Esprit programming
  4. 04

    Stamping

    Prototype dies and low or high volume runs on presses from 60 to 250 tons, used for die tryout and production. Long experience running progressive dies, with die repair available as required.

    • 60 to 250 ton presses
    • Progressive dies
    • Prototype dies
  5. 05

    Jig grinding, trim dies and fixtures

    Jig grinding, trim dies, prototypes and complete design and build of custom tools, dies and fixtures, all done in house on modern machinery.

How a job moves through the shop

From print to shipped part

  1. 1

    Design

    2D or 3D die design in AutoCAD, DIEMAKER and Solid Edge.

  2. 2

    Machine

    CNC machining, wire EDM and jig grinding of die components.

  3. 3

    Build and debug

    Assembly and debug by trained die makers.

  4. 4

    Tryout

    Die tryout in our own presses up to 250 tons.

  5. 5

    Run production

    Low or high volume stamping, with die maintenance.
