About Us

Manufacturer of Hospital Products and Accessories

Founded in 1982, Unitec Hospitalar has become one of the most traditional companies in the market. It specializes in manufacturing products for the medical and hospital sector, with a focus on oxygen therapy, inhalation therapy, anesthesia and intensive care, and its main priority is serving customers quickly and effectively.

Headquartered in São Paulo, the company has distributors throughout Brazil and in South American countries, and keeps expanding its range of equipment, gaining a larger share and recognition in the hospital market.

Unitec continuously invests in technology and works to meet every requirement for manufacturing its products. This shows in its compliance with the highest manufacturing and quality standards, strictly following all the rules set by the responsible bodies, such as ANVISA and INMETRO.

In 2018 Unitec Hospitalar earned the certification of its Quality Management System to ABNT NBR ISO 9001:2015 and ABNT NBR ISO 13485:2016.

With more than 100 products in its wide manufacturing line, such as portable emergency units, modular panels for beds and ICUs, alarm panels, manifold systems, suction units, flowmeters, valves, humidifiers, nebulizers, wall outlets and accessories, Unitec is a reference in the country's main hospitals.
