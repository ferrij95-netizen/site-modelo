Capabilities

Capabilities

01

Tube mill cut-off tooling

One of our main products: die jaws, die guides and complete die sets for the cut-off area, including rework and sharpening.

  • Die jaws
  • Die guides
  • Complete die sets
  • Rework and sharpening
02

Tube mill parts

Mill shafts, dedent plugs, strippers, mandrels, star wheels, tally wheels, stands, spacers, strip guides, entry tables and many other parts made to customer specs.

  • Mill shafts
  • Mandrels
  • Star and tally wheels
  • Strip guides
  • Entry tables
03

Checking fixtures and gages

We design and build attribute and SPC checking fixtures for automotive, glass container manufacturing and other industries, designed per GD&T and customer specs. All automotive fixtures are certified by an independent third-party A2LA certified lab or equivalent.

  • Attribute fixtures
  • SPC fixtures
  • GD&T design
  • A2LA certification
04

Special machinery

Custom machines such as tube punching machines, designed in solid modeling and built in our shop.

05

CNC machined components and fabrication

Multi-axis CNC machined components and custom fabrications for pharmaceutical, energy and many other industries, all programmed with CAM software.
