About us

A family job shop since 1973

Select Tool is a small, family-operated job shop located in Toledo, Ohio, that started in 1973. Our 15,000 sq ft facility and wide array of machinery allow us to handle small jobs and production runs as well.

We use CAM software to program all of our CNC machinery and solid modeling for all of our designs, and every automotive fixture we build is certified by an independent third-party lab.

Why customers choose Select

  • Tube mill know-how

    Decades of cut-off tooling, rework and sharpening.

  • Certified fixtures

    GD&T design and A2LA third-party certification.

  • Family service

    You talk to the people who run the shop.
