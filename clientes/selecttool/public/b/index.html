Checking fixtures and gages

We design and build attribute and SPC checking fixtures for automotive, glass container manufacturing and other industries, designed per GD&T and customer specs. All automotive fixtures are certified by an independent third-party A2LA certified lab or equivalent.

  • Attribute fixtures
  • SPC fixtures
  • GD&T design
  • A2LA certification

Who we are

Small jobs and production runs, handled with care.

Select Tool is a small, family-operated job shop in Toledo, Ohio, started in 1973. Our 15,000 sq ft facility and wide array of machinery let us handle small jobs and production runs alike.

We serve the tube mill, automotive and glass container industries, among many others, with CAM programming on all of our CNC machinery and solid modeling for all of our designs.
