Sustainability

Sustainability: that’s AB!

See our 2024 Sustainability Report and the commitments we have made to build a more sustainable future.

2027 commitment

Our commitment for 2027: 40% of the resin processed at AB will be post-consumer recycled.

Carbon Neutral

We earned the Carbon Neutral Company certificate by offsetting the gases we emit directly and indirectly.

Renewable energy

The energy used in our operations comes from 100% renewable sources.

Zero Waste

With full process waste management, we are a certified Zero Waste company, sending over 95% of our waste to sustainable destinations.

Rainwater reuse

Rainwater supplies our restrooms, about 10% of the operation’s total water use.

Composting program

Since 2024, about 20% of the cafeteria’s organic waste goes to composting.

Waste sorting

Recycling rate above 90% for 4 years. Average annual collection, including production waste, exceeds 440 tonnes.

Drop-off point

Drop-off point for batteries and used cooking oil. Since 2022, over 200 liters of oil a year no longer go to improper disposal.

Sustainable Development Goals

ODS

The Sustainable Development Goals are a global call to end poverty, protect the environment and the climate and ensure people can enjoy peace and prosperity. AB contributes to SDGs 3, 4, 9, 11 and 12.

Social responsibility

Instituto A Fonte da Alegria

The A Fonte da Alegria Institute runs actions to improve the quality of life of families and the community and raises social awareness among employees, who can volunteer. Its projects have 3 pillars: Creating Memories (elderly), Inspiring Tomorrow (children’s education) and Nourishing Generations (food and supplement donations).
