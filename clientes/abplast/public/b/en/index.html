How we make it

From idea to production, full support for your project.

We rely on certified quality processes and a complete team of specialists in many bottle and packaging production methods, such as extrusion blow molding, injection, ISBM-PET and decoration on PET, PP and PE. As part of the Catarinense Pharma group, we share experience, quality and purpose in every delivery.

  1. 01

    ISBM-PET

    PET packaging with high mechanical strength, high gloss and transparency.

  2. 02

    Blow molding

    More flexible (squeeze) packaging, with finishing options and post-consumer recycled resins.

  3. 03

    Injection

    Processing of resins such as PE and PP.

  4. 04

    Decoration

    Screen printing, labeling, pad printing, hot-stamping and sleeves.
