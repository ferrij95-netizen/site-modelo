Capabilities

How we build a mold

All capabilities
01

Mold design and engineering

Our engineering and design department uses the latest in 3D solid modeling. With SolidWorks and Pro/ENGINEER we deliver precision 3D models that become fully functional molds. Our engineers are experienced in complex mold designs that meet the demands of your product.

  • 3D solid modeling
  • SolidWorks and Pro/ENGINEER
  • Complex mold design
02

High-speed machining

Makino high-performance mills up to 32,000 RPM and graphite milling machines cut steel and electrodes with a finish that needs little or no bench work. Mastercam 3D CAM takes the model straight to the machine.

  • Makino Hyper 5, V33
  • Graphite electrode milling
  • Mastercam 3D CAM
03

CNC EDM

Sinker EDM with automatic tool changers, wire EDM and hole drilling EDM for the fine detail, sharp corners and deep ribs that molds for connectors and medical parts require.

  • Sinker EDM, 16-position changers
  • Wire EDM
  • EDM hole drilling
04

Precision grinding

A full grinding department, from wet surface grinders to cylindrical grinding, for mold components that fit right the first time.

  • Wet surface grinding
  • Surface grinding
  • Cylindrical grinding
05

Prototype to production

From rapid prototyping to high-production tools, we build molds for multifaceted parts in every resin and color, including insert molds for terminal strips.

  • Rapid prototype molds
  • High-production molds
  • Insert molding tools

Who we are

A distinctive tool shop, with a loyal team behind every mold.

With more than six decades of manufacturing history, Tool-All has earned a position at the top of the tooling industry. By embracing technological evolution, we have succeeded in an ever-changing, highly skilled molding business.

Using solid modeling and state-of-the-art CNC equipment, we have increased productivity and eliminated part variation. From rapid prototyping to high production, our design engineers and accomplished craftsmen work together to bring your product to market without delay. A visit is always welcome.
