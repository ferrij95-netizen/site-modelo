About us

Erie mold makers since 1962

Tool-All Inc. has been supplying precision molds since 1962. Over the decades we embraced each new technology, from CNC to high-speed machining and solid modeling, and that is how we stayed at the top of a demanding trade.

Visit us and you will see a distinctive tool shop, with a loyal group of people working together as a team, providing the industry with an outstanding source of precision tooling.
