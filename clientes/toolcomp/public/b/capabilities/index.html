Milling

Grinding

CNC and manual grinding: universal grinding, surface grinding and honing, and chrome grinding.

  • Surface grinding and honing

Turning

CNC lathes for small batch production up to 5,000 parts, prototype turnings, single-piece turnings and hard metals above 60 Rockwell.

  • Prototypes
  • Hard metals 60+ HRC
