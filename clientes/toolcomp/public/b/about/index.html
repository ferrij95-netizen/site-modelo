About us

Toledo machinists since 1993

Tooling & Components Corp. was founded in 1993, established as a tool and machining company to serve the tool and die industry of Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan.

In 2007, after the near collapse of the domestic automakers, Toolcomp diversified into other fields of machining, supplying key components for hydraulic, valve, pump, compressor and turbine applications.
