Who we are

Built for the tool and die industry. Ready for yours.

Tooling & Components Corp. was founded in 1993 as a tool and machining company serving the tool and die industry of Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan. We specialize in both CNC and manual machining: milling, grinding, turning and drilling.

In 2007, after the near collapse of the domestic automakers, Toolcomp diversified into hydrostatic hydraulic components, valve, pump, compressor and turbine parts. With specially trained machinists and a diverse collection of manual and CNC machines, we can complete most small to large machining projects.
